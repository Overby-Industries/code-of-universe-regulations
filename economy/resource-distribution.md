# Resource Distribution

*How a resource moves from extraction or production to a being who needs
it — the ISRU waterfall, the Commons Reserve, and the being-level guarantee
that makes the whole system something other than a payroll.*

- **Status:** Draft
- **Sources:** `pddc-treasury-and-funding.md`, `foundation/cur-foundation-012.md` (CREF §4),
  `titles/CUR-X/cur-x-part-4.md` §4.6

## Why this file exists

`PDDC-TREASURY-AND-FUNDING.md` states how the Commonwealth, as an
institution, collects and allocates ISRU revenue. CUR-X.4 §4.6 states, from
the opposite end, the rule that makes any of it meaningful to an individual
being: access is never conditioned on contribution. The two documents
govern the same system at different altitudes and neither cites the other.
This file connects them. It states no new allocation and creates no new
entitlement; where it summarises, the source document governs.

## The institutional side: where ISRU revenue goes

Every unit of gross ISRU revenue is distributed in a fixed order. No step
may be skipped, and no step may be reduced to fund a later one:

1. **Commons Reserve — 20%.** The floor PDDC §9.3(a) establishes, taken
   before anything else.
2. **Environmental Remediation Fund — 3%.**
3. **Workers' Dividend Pool — 5%.** See [`incentives.md`](incentives.md) for
   what this actually is and is not.
4. **License Holder Returns — the remainder.**

*(`PDDC-TREASURY-AND-FUNDING.md` §3.3)*

**The Commons Reserve itself subdivides**, and none of the floors below may
be reduced except by a Type B constitutional amendment:

| Sub-account | Floor | Funds |
|---|---|---|
| Life-Sustaining Infrastructure Fund | 40% of Commons Reserve receipts | Infrastructure essential to survival, health, habitat |
| Universal Basic Provisions Fund | 20% | Universal access to food, water, shelter, basic communications |
| RALC Obligations Fund | 15% | Commonwealth fulfilment of RALC obligations to all Recognized Life-Forms |
| Long-Term Civilizational Investment Fund | 15% | Research, education, culture, long-horizon infrastructure |
| Protected Mode Continuity Reserve | 10% | Continuity during Protected Mode — 90 days of disbursements, held separately, drawn only for life-sustaining purposes |

*(§5.1)*

**Deficit and surplus do not touch these floors.** A Moderate or Material
Deficit triggers a remediation plan that must achieve structural balance
without reducing any mandatory floor; a surplus is swept toward the
Continuity Reserve first, then the Long-Term Investment and Universal Basic
Provisions Funds, and never distributed to individual Members as a dividend.
*(§8.2(c), §9.1)*

## The being-level side: what this actually guarantees you

**None of the above is a payroll, and access to what it funds does not run
through the waterfall at all.** Access to a Vital Continuity Service — the
Life Support, Knowledge, Infrastructure, and Economic Participation
guarantees CREF §4 lists in full — is never conditioned on contribution,
employment, or any assessment of desert, for any being, without exception.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.6(a)–(b), CREF §4)*

**This is the load-bearing distinction.** The Treasury Assembly's waterfall
governs how the Commonwealth *funds* the Life-Sustaining Infrastructure and
Universal Basic Provisions Funds. CUR-X.4 §4.6 governs whether *your* access
to what those funds pay for depends on anything you've done. It does not.
The two questions have different answers and are decided by different
documents — confusing "how is this funded" with "what do I have to do to
get it" is exactly the error CUR-X.4 §4.6(c) names directly: an arrangement
in which beings work for resources becomes forced labour the moment access
is conditioned on the work, and the Commons Reserve waterfall never
conditions it.

## What crosses in from outside

Where a resource is acquired through External Trade under CUR-X.6, the
Currency Boundary is crossed once, at acquisition — the resource is
thereafter allocated under CUR-X.4 §4.6 on the same terms as anything
produced internally, and the price paid for it does not travel forward with
it. *(CUR-X.6 §6.4(c))* See [`trade-policy.md`](trade-policy.md) for the
boundary itself.

## Monitoring

The Commonwealth continuously monitors availability across every guaranteed
category CREF §4 lists — food, housing, healthcare, education, power, and
the rest — and a deficiency in any of them requires corrective action,
independent of whether anyone has complained. *(CREF §20)*
