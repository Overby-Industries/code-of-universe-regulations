# Trade Policy

*How the Aevoric Commonwealth deals with a party that isn't part of it —
CUR-X.6's currency boundary and the Treasury's own external-revenue rules,
read together.*

- **Status:** Draft
- **Sources:** `titles/CUR-X/cur-x-part-6.md`, `pddc-treasury-and-funding.md` §4.2–§4.3, §13

## Why this file exists

CUR-X.6 governs the boundary itself — what may cross it, in which
direction, and what may never cross it at all. `PDDC-TREASURY-AND-FUNDING.md`
governs the Commonwealth's own institutional authority to enter external
agreements in the first place. The two were drafted separately and don't
cite each other; this file is where they meet. It states no new rule; where
it summarises, the source document governs.

## The boundary is institutional, not personal

**External Trade is conducted by the Commonwealth, a Commonwealth
institution, or an authorised enterprise — never by a being on their own
account with Commonwealth resources.** *(CUR-X.6 §6.3(a))* This is the rule
everything else on this page depends on.

**A being's own external dealings, in their personal capacity, are outside
this Part entirely.** You may hold, receive, spend, or use external
currency in dealings with an external party at your own choice — the
Commonwealth does not reach that. What it reaches is currency an
institution or enterprise receives in an institutional transaction, and
whether that currency is allowed to cross back into the internal allocation
system. *(§6.3(d))*

## What external currency may be used for

Only three things: acquiring something the Commonwealth cannot produce or
allocate internally; discharging an obligation owed to an external party;
or holding it in reserve against one of those two needs. *(CUR-X.6 §6.4(a))*

**Once a resource acquired this way enters the Commonwealth, it is
allocated exactly like anything produced internally.** The boundary is
crossed once, at acquisition — the price paid for it does not travel
forward into the internal system with it. *(§6.4(c))* See
[`resource-distribution.md`](resource-distribution.md).

## What may never cross, in either direction

**No mechanism may let a being earn external currency for internal conduct,
or spend it on an internal necessity.** Either alone recreates paid labour
or rationing-by-wealth through a side door; both together do it while the
internal rule "access is not conditioned on contribution" stays nominally
true. *(CUR-X.6 §6.5(a)–(c))*

**A being's standing, participation, or access under the Code is never
affected by whether they engage in external trade, or by what external
currency they personally hold.** *(§6.5(d))*

**If an institution or enterprise breaches this, the measure reaches its
authorisation — never the beings who received the distribution.** *(§6.5(e))*

## The Treasury's authority to trade at all

CUR-X.6 governs the boundary once a transaction happens; the Treasury
Assembly's own authority to enter one is separately constrained:

- **External revenue agreements** (grants, revenue sharing, CUR-member
  organisation agreements) require CC negotiation, TA majority vote, and GA
  resolution. *(`pddc-treasury-and-funding.md` §4.2(b))*
- **None may** grant an external entity governance rights over Commonwealth
  institutions, require any being to waive RALC rights, create obligations
  that would constitute a forbidden transition, or subordinate any
  Commonwealth financial function to external control. *(§4.2(c))*
- **Grants and donations** may not carry governance conditions, naming
  rights over public institutions, or preferential treatment for the donor
  in any Commonwealth proceeding, and are published above a TA-set
  threshold. *(§4.3)*
- **CUR-level assessments** for inter-organisational activity are a
  mandatory budget line item, but none may require reducing a mandatory
  expenditure floor, drawing the Protected Mode Continuity Reserve, or
  granting CUR governance rights over Commonwealth finances. *(§13.1)*

## Transparency

Every External Trade transaction above a Treasury-set threshold is
published under CUR-X.5 §5.2, naming the institution or enterprise involved
(never a being acting personally) and recording the currency exchanged and
its purpose under §6.4(a). The Commonwealth's aggregate external-currency
holding is published quarterly, so a growing reserve is visible before it
is large enough to function as the informal wage fund §6.5 forbids it
becoming. *(CUR-X.6 §6.6)*

## No emergency exception

A resource shortage that external trade could relieve is addressed by the
Commonwealth acquiring the resource and allocating it internally under
CUR-X.4 §4.6 — not by letting beings acquire external currency directly to
purchase relief for themselves. The first keeps access independent of
currency; the second is the boundary failing exactly when it matters most.
*(CUR-X.6 §6.8)*
