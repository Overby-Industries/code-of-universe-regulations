# Incentives

*What forms of recognition for contribution actually exist in the Aevoric
Commonwealth — and the hard limits that keep this page short.*

- **Status:** Draft
- **Sources:** `titles/CUR-X/cur-x-part-4.md` §4.6, §4.10, `pddc-treasury-and-funding.md` §3.4,
  `progressive-direct-democracy-constitution.md` §18.4

## Read this first

**This document cannot describe an incentive system in the ordinary sense,
because the Code forecloses one.** CUR-X.4 §4.6(e) permits contribution to
be "recognised, thanked, recorded, and celebrated" and states in the same
sentence that recognition "may not take the form of differential access to
necessities." §4.10(b)(7) separately voids, without exception, "differential
treatment of beings on the basis of their contribution, productivity, or
economic usefulness." A rewards system, a points system, or a tiered-access
scheme would be exactly that. This page states what remains once those
limits are taken seriously, rather than working around them — working
around them is the specific failure §4.10(c) names: every mechanism this
page could otherwise propose has historically been introduced by people who
meant well, and meaning well was never the safeguard.

## What exists

**The Workers' Dividend.** Distributed annually to everyone who performed
ISRU work in the preceding fiscal year, funded from 5% of gross ISRU revenue
under [`resource-distribution.md`](resource-distribution.md)'s waterfall.
At least 60% of the pool is distributed equal per-capita to every eligible
worker; the Workers' Dividend Representative proposes the formula for the
remainder annually, subject to Treasury Assembly approval. *(`pddc-treasury-and-funding.md`
§3.4)* This is compensation for work actually performed inside a specific,
bounded economic activity (ISRU) — not a general merit system, and it does
not touch access to any Vital Continuity Service, which remains
unconditional regardless of whether a being has ever done ISRU work at all.

**The Ascending Arc Medal.** The Commonwealth's highest civic honour,
awarded by General Assembly vote to an Avian or Recognized Life-Form for
extraordinary contribution to the Commonwealth's Ascending Principle. It may
not be awarded posthumously, and may not go to a sitting Constitutional
Court or Capture Tribunal Justice or a current CC executive officeholder —
the people positioned to influence who receives it are excluded from
receiving it. Recipients are named in a permanent public registry.
*(`progressive-direct-democracy-constitution.md` §18.4)* This is recognition
in exactly the form §4.6(e) permits: it is thanked and celebrated, and it
confers no differential access to anything.

**Ordinary recognition.** Thanks, public credit, and record of what someone
did remain open at any scale, informally and institutionally, provided none
of it is required and none of it gates a necessity. *(CUR-X.4 §4.6(e)–(f))*

## What does not exist, and will not be added here

- **No contribution score.** CUR-X.4 §4.6(d)(5) prohibits maintaining any
  record of contribution for the purpose of determining access, and CUR-H.2
  §2.5(g) prohibits secret scoring generally.
- **No tiered access to necessities based on productivity.** §4.10(b)(7).
- **No currency earnable by internal contribution and spendable on internal
  necessities.** CUR-X.6 §6.5 forecloses exactly this as the mechanism by
  which a currency system re-enters an economy that has foreclosed it at
  the front door.
- **No enterprise-level reward scheme that functions as differential
  access.** CUR-X.4 §4.3(d) makes the underlying rights non-waivable, so an
  enterprise cannot recreate through incentive design what it could not
  impose directly.

## If a real proposal wants to add something here

It has to answer one question before anything else: does receiving more of
this, or less of it, change any being's access to a Vital Continuity
Service, directly or through a record that could later be used that way? If
the answer is yes, or genuinely unclear, §4.6 and §4.10(b)(7) already
resolve it, and no proposal can change that outcome without a Type A
Entrenched constitutional process neither this page nor an ordinary
amendment can reach. If the answer is a clean no, it belongs in this
document as a third example alongside the two above.
