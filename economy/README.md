None of the three states a rule `pddc-treasury-and-funding.md`, CUR-X.4, or
CUR-X.6 does not already state. Where they summarise, the source document
governs. Two rules apply to all three and are worth stating once, here,
rather than in each file separately:

**Access to a Vital Continuity Service is never conditioned on
contribution, employment, productivity, or any assessment of desert. This
is absolute and reaches every document in this folder.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.6,
CREF §4)*

**No provision here may be read to create emergency financial authority.**
PDDC §12.6 and `pddc-treasury-and-funding.md` §6.4 both state this
independently; neither yields to the other, and neither yields to anything
in this folder.
