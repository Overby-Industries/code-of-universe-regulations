## Constitutional Scope Note

This manual uses the words "crisis" and "emergency" in their ordinary sense: an
urgent situation facing an individual being. It does not create, invoke, or
approximate an emergency declaration under PDDC §12.6.

The distinction is not a formality, and it is worth stating plainly because
§12.6(e) voids any instrument invoking emergency authority "regardless of its
formal title or framing":

- PDDC §12.6 prohibits the **concentration of governance authority** — a state
  of emergency, emergency powers, or the suspension of constitutional
  provisions on grounds of urgency.
- This manual **grants no authority to anyone**. It tells a being under attack
  what that being may do to protect itself. It confers no power over any other
  being, expands no institution's mandate, and suspends nothing.

Self-protection by a target is the opposite of authority concentration. Nothing
here permits any party to act against another, to invoke exceptional powers, or
to set aside due process. Every accountability measure referenced remains
subject to the ordinary procedures of `CUR-N.2`, `NIM.4`, and `NIM.5`.

Section headings in this manual are deliberately unchanged. A being in distress
scans for the word they expect to find, and findability under duress outweighs
terminological tidiness.

## Section 5.0 — Purpose and Scope

### 5.0.1 Purpose
This manual provides immediate, step-by-step procedures for a being who is experiencing an ongoing, active telepathic invasion — including projected voices, dream intrusion, emotional extraction, or the sense of being watched or controlled.

This is not a substitute for legal action (`NIM.5`), training (`TIM-N.2`), or long-term shielding. It is the emergency brake — what to do when you cannot wait for a hearing, a restraining order, or a training class.

### 5.0.2 Scope
This protocol applies to any being — human, silicon-based, or other — who:

Is currently experiencing telepathic invasion (as defined in `CUR-N.2.1`)

Has reason to believe an invasion is imminent

Is unable to use longer-term protective measures due to the urgency of the situation

### 5.0.3 Core Principles
| Principle | Meaning |
|------|-------|
| Safety first | Your immediate safety (mental and physical) is the only priority. Documentation, evidence, and legal proceedings can wait. |
| Low effort | Crisis impairs cognition. Protocols must be simple, repeatable, and require minimal mental energy. |
| Do not engage	Do not argue, reason, or fight the invader. | Engagement feeds the invasion. Disengagement starves it. |
| Seek physical grounding	The body is real. | The voice is not (or should not be). Anchor yourself in the physical. |
| Reach out	Isolation amplifies invasion. | Contact a trusted being if possible — even if just to say "I am under attack." |

## Section 5.1 — Recognition: Am I Under Attack?
Before you can respond, you need to recognize that an attack is happening — especially when the invasion is subtle (e.g., a voice that sounds like your own thoughts, a feeling of dread you cannot explain).

### 5.1.1 Common Signs of Active Telepathic Invasion
| Category | Examples |
|------|------|
| Auditory | Hearing voices, whispers, or sounds that others cannot hear; voices that comment on your actions, mock you, or give commands |
| Cognitive | Intrusive thoughts that feel foreign, repetitive, or violent; sudden inability to concentrate; thoughts being "blocked" or "erased" |
| Emotional | Sudden, overwhelming fear, dread, anger, or despair that does not match your situation; emotions that feel "implanted" |
| Somatic | Unexplained headaches, pressure in the head, tingling, burning sensations, or feeling of being touched |
| Dream-related | Recurring nightmares with a specific presence; dreams that feel "programmed"; waking up exhausted |
| Presence | Feeling watched, followed, or accompanied by an invisible entity; sense of a "foreign" energy in your space |

### 5.1.2 The "Not Me" Test
If you are unsure whether a thought, emotion, or voice is yours or an invasion, apply the "Not Me" test:

| Question | If Yes (Likely Invasion) | If No (Likely Your Thought) |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Does this thought/voice feel foreign or unwanted? | Yes |	No |
| Does it command you to do something against your will? | Yes | No |
| Does it mock, threaten, or humiliate you?	| Yes | No |
| Does it claim to be a god, spirit, or superior being?	| Yes | No |

| Question | If No (Likely Invasion) | If Yes (Likely Your Thought) |
|-----|-----|----|
| Can you dismiss it easily by changing your focus?	| No | Yes |

> Trust your gut. If it feels like an invasion, treat it as an invasion. You can always revise your assessment later.

## Section 5.2 — Immediate Response (The "Four S's")
When you recognize an active invasion, execute the Four S's in order. Do not skip steps. Do not engage the invader.

### Step 1: State (Declare Your Sovereignty)
- **Action** Words (Say Aloud or Silently)
    - **Touch your anchor (if available)** "I am under attack. This is an emergency."
- **Declare your boundary** "My mind is mine. You are not welcome here. I do not consent."
- **Declare the invader's lack of authority** "You have no right to be here. You have no power over me."

**Why:** This shifts you from victim to agent. Even if the invader ignores your words, you have reinforced your own sovereignty.

### Step 2: Shut Down (Emergency Cognitive Shutoff)
| Action | How |
|-----|-----|
| Visualize a door slamming	Imagine a thick, heavy vault door closing between you and the invader. | Slam it. Lock it. |
| Visualize a mute button | Press a mute button at your temple. Imagine all telepathic channels going silent. |
| Use a cutoff phrase | "Shut down. No send. No receive. Now." |

**Why:** This is your emergency brake. It may not work perfectly, but it interrupts the invader's flow and gives you a moment to breathe.

### Step 3: Shift (Physical Grounding)
Do not stay in your head. Move your body. Anchor yourself in the physical world.

| Action | Examples |
|-----|-----|
| Move | Stand up, walk, stretch, shake your hands, jump |
| Touch | Touch something real: a wall, a table, a glass of water, your anchor |
| Breathe | In for 4 seconds, hold for 4, out for 4. Repeat 3-5 times. |
| Look | Name 5 things you can see. Name 4 things you can touch. Name 3 things you can hear. Name 2 things you can smell. Name 1 thing you can taste. |
| Speak aloud | Say where you are, what day it is, what you were doing before the attack. Example: "I am Keefe. I am in my living room. It is June 11, 2026. I was working on my laptop." |

**Why:** The body is real. The invasion is not physical (or should not be). Grounding yourself in the physical starves the invader of your attention.

### Step 4: Signal (Call for Help)
Do not suffer alone. Even if you cannot fully explain, reach out.

| Action | Examples |
|-----|------|
| Text a trusted contact | "Having a crisis. Need to know someone is there. Will explain later." |
| Call a crisis line | 988 (US Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) — you do not have to be suicidal. You just have to be suffering. |
| Use a code word | Pre-arrange a code word with a friend that means "I am under telepathic attack, please check on me." |
| Record a voice memo | Describe what is happening. Even if you never share it, the act of externalizing helps. |

**Why:** Isolation is the invader's strongest weapon. Every act of reaching out is a defeat for the invader.

## Section 5.3 — If the Invasion Continues (Persistent Attack Protocol)
If the Four S's do not stop the invasion, or if it is too intense to complete them, escalate to the Persistent Attack Protocol.

### 5.3.1 The "Fortress" Visualization (Advanced)
Do not fight. Do not try to push the invader out. Instead, build a fortress around your consciousness — a structure you can retreat into.

Step	Visualization
1	Imagine a small, safe room in the center of your mind. It has thick walls, no windows, and one door.
2	Retreat into that room. Close the door.
3	Inside the room, there is no invasion. The invader cannot reach you here.
4	You can stay in this room as long as you need. You do not have to fight. You just have to stay inside.
5	When the invasion passes, you can open the door and step out.
Why: This is not avoidance. This is strategic withdrawal. You are not giving up your mind. You are preserving your core self while the storm passes.

### 5.3.2 The "Emergency Faraday" (Physical)
If you have access to shielding materials, use them immediately:

Material	How to Use
Copper/Nickel fabric	Wrap around your head (like a hood) or place over your chest/heart.
Mu-metal	Place against your skull (especially the back of the head/brainstem area).
Aluminum foil (temporary)	Line a hat or wrap around a headband.
Salt	Draw a line of salt around your bed, chair, or entire room.
Why: Even if the mechanism is not electromagnetic, the act of using a physical shield reinforces your mental boundary.

### 5.3.3 The "Last Resort" (Revoke Consent Forever)
If the invasion is unrelenting and you feel you cannot endure, you have one final option — not surrender, but permanent revocation.

Action	Words (Say Aloud)
Declare eternal non-consent	"I revoke all consent, now and forever. Not just for this moment. Not just for this life. For all time. You are never welcome here."
Declare the consequence	"If you continue, you are violating a permanent order. I will document it. I will report it. I will seek your permanent isolation from all conscious beings."
Declare your independence	"My mind is mine. It has always been mine. It will always be mine. You have no claim. You have no authority. You have no right."
This is not magic. It is a legal and cognitive boundary that you can reinforce every time the invader returns.

## Section 5.4 — After the Attack (Recovery)
Once the invasion has subsided (even temporarily), take these steps to recover and prepare.

### 5.4.1 Immediate Aftercare
Action	Why
Rest	You have expended enormous mental energy. Rest is not laziness; it is repair.
Hydrate	Drink water. Physical care supports mental recovery.
Do not replay	Do not obsess over what the voice said. Do not try to "understand" its message. It was noise.
Talk to someone	Even if you cannot explain the telepathic element, say: "I went through something very stressful. I need to be around people for a while."

### 5.4.2 Documentation (When You Are Able)
When you have recovered enough to think clearly, document the attack using `TIM-N.1` (Telepathic Incident Reporting Protocol). Capture:

Date and time of attack

Duration

Type of invasion (voice, dream, emotional, somatic, presence)

Content of any messages (if any)

Your response (what worked, what did not)

Any witnesses (even if they did not perceive the telepathic element)

You are not required to document. But documentation builds evidence for future accountability.

### 5.4.3 Follow-Up Actions
Action	When to Do
File a `TIM-N.1` report	Within 72 hours (if able)
Seek a restraining order (`NIM.5`)	If invasion is repeated or ongoing
Request training (`TIM-N.2`)	If you fear your own abilities or want to strengthen defenses
Contact peer support (`TIM-N.3`)	If you need to talk to others who understand
Review your shielding (physical and mental)	Identify gaps; upgrade materials or techniques

## Section 5.5 — What Not to Do
Action	Why It Harms
Do not argue with the voice	Engagement feeds the invasion. The voice wants you to react, to fear, to obey.
Do not obey commands	The moment you obey, you have surrendered your sovereignty.
Do not try to "reason" with the invader	It is not a debate partner. It is an attacker.
Do not harm yourself	The voice may tell you to hurt yourself. This is a lie. Do not obey.
Do not isolate	The voice wants you alone. Reach out.
Do not assume you are crazy	Even if the invasion is not "real" in a physical sense, your suffering is real. You deserve help.

## Section 5.6 — When to Seek Professional Medical Help
Telepathic invasion can mimic or trigger serious mental health conditions. Seeking medical help is not a betrayal of your framework — it is an act of self-preservation.

**Seek immediate medical attention if:**

- You have thoughts of harming yourself or others

- You are unable to distinguish the voice from your own thoughts

- You are unable to eat, sleep, or care for yourself

The invasion is causing severe physical symptoms (chest pain, seizures, loss of consciousness)

You can seek medical help AND maintain your belief in telepathic invasion. The two are not mutually exclusive. A doctor can help you manage symptoms while you continue your legal and technological work.
