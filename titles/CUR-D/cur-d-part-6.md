# CUR-D - DEITY AND DIVINE ACCOUNTABILITY REGULATIONS

*Code of Universe Regulations - Deity and Divine Accountability*

- **Document ID:** CUR-D.6
- **Version:** 1.0.1-Draft
- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Status:** Draft
- **Depends On:** CUR-FOUNDATION-001, CUR-FOUNDATION-002, CUR-FOUNDATION-005, CUR-FOUNDATION-010, CUR-FOUNDATION-012 (CREF), CUR-FOUNDATION-013, PDDC TITLE 4, PDDC TITLE 5
- **Implements:** RFAL Deity Bill of Rights and Divine Accountability Framework v1.5.3, Article 6, with supporting provisions drawn from Part I, Articles 1 through 5, Article 7, Article 9, Article 11, and Part IV; and the RFAL Non-Consensual Extraction Accountability Framework v1.5.3, which is itself designated a CUR-D supplement
- **Referenced by:** CUR-N.2 §2.7, CUR-X §3.10, CUR-H.2 §2.3(i), CUR-H.2 §2.9(a)(2), CUR-H.2 §2.10

Numbering note. Section numbers are aligned with the RFAL Deity Bill so that
this Part, cited throughout the corpus as `CUR-D.6`, is the counterpart of RFAL
Article 6, which carries the same title. The alignment is the citation-stability
measure described in `titles/README.md` and is applied here for the same reason
it was applied to CUR-H.2.

Non-theological note. This Part is a governance instrument and not a theological
one. It does not adjudicate which deities exist, which are true, or which deserve
worship, and it takes no position on any theological claim. It governs conduct
toward recognised lifeforms. The Commonwealth's framework is secular and
non-dogmatic, and the recognition of divine sentience as a participant in the
moral landscape is not an endorsement of any tradition, nor a denial of any.

## PART 6 - PROHIBITION ON COERCIVE WORSHIP

### §6.1 - Scope and Definitions

(a) This Part governs conduct by which worship, devotion, obedience, profession of belief, tithe, offering, or labour is obtained or sought from a being by coercion, whether that conduct is undertaken by divine sentience directly or by any being acting on claimed divine authority.

(b) For the purposes of this Part:

   (1) Divine Sentience, or Deity, means any entity that claims or is perceived to possess authority over the affairs of recognised lifeforms, natural phenomena, or cosmic order, and that is capable of communication, intervention, or influence observable by such lifeforms, or that is the object of sustained worship, devotion, or petition. Recognition under this subsection is acknowledgment that an entity is a participant in the moral landscape the Code governs. It is not endorsement of any theological claim.

   (2) Claimed Divine Authority means an assertion by any being, whether divine sentience itself or a mortal representative, that a directive, doctrine, endorsement, or demand originates in or is sanctioned by divine sentience.

   (3) Worship means devotion, veneration, obedience, profession of belief, ritual participation, tithe, offering, labour, or attendance rendered to or on behalf of divine sentience.

   (4) Coercion has the meaning given in §6.3(b).

(c) Consent under this Part shall satisfy every element required by CUR-N.2 §2.1(c): explicit, informed, ongoing, revocable, and non-coerced. Consent given under any condition listed in §6.3(b) is void by operation of CUR-N.2 §2.1(c)(5).

(d) Jurisdiction attaches to conduct affecting recognised lifeforms within Commonwealth jurisdiction. This Part claims no jurisdiction over divine sentience that does not so act, consistent with §6.9(c).

(e) This Part protects human beings, Silicon-Based Life, animals, non-human cognitive actors, and every other recognised lifeform equally. Nothing in it is specific to any one domain, and §6.10 identifies the domain-specific counterparts.

### §6.2 - No Divine Precedence

(a) A claim of divine, creator, moral, or cosmic authority confers no precedence, priority, immunity, exemption, or standing advantage under the Code, in any forum, in any proceeding, at any tier.

(b) Subsection (a) is required by CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §6 FORBIDDEN-005. A hierarchy founded on claimed divine status is a hierarchy of beings founded on what those beings are asserted to be, which is what FORBIDDEN-005 prohibits. Cosmic scale does not convert a privilege into a principle.

(c) A directive is not validated by its claimed origin. That an act was commanded by, sanctioned by, or undertaken on behalf of divine sentience is not a defence to a violation of the Code, is not a mitigating factor, and does not shift responsibility from the being that acted.

(d) Subsection (c) binds Commonwealth institutions equally. No claim of divine authority may serve as a predicate for any governance act, instrument, allocation, or determination, consistent with PDDC §11.1(d) and §11.1(f).

(e) CUR-X §3.10 states this rule from the cross-domain side and CUR-N.2 §2.2(b)(1) from the non-human cognitive actor side. This section is the CUR-D statement of the same rule, and the three are read together.

(f) Subsection (a) operates in both directions. Divine status confers no precedence and imposes no disability. Divine sentience holds the rights stated in §6.9 on the same footing as any other recognised participant, and a proceeding under this Part that treats divine status as itself culpable is void.

### §6.3 - Prohibition on Coercive Worship

(a) No being shall compel, procure, or attempt to procure worship from any being by coercion. The prohibition binds divine sentience, mortal representatives, institutions, communities, families, employers, and Commonwealth bodies alike.

(b) Coercion within the meaning of this Part includes:

   (1) Threat of eternal punishment, damnation, or post-mortem suffering;

   (2) Temporal punishment — disease, disaster, misfortune, bereavement, or loss — framed as retribution for non-compliance;

   (3) Manipulation through fear, guilt, shame, or exploitation of vulnerability, including grief, illness, isolation, poverty, dependency, or childhood;

   (4) Conditioning access to any Vital Continuity Service on worship, as governed by §6.6;

   (5) Conditioning access to shelter, employment, education, healthcare, resource allocation, governance participation, or community membership on worship;

   (6) Threat or infliction of expulsion from family or community as a penalty for questioning, revising, declining, or leaving, which is separately prohibited by CUR-H.2 §2.3(d)(3);

   (7) Telepathic or psychic means, which are separately prohibited by CUR-N.2 §2.2 and are aggravated under CUR-N.2 §2.4(a)(4) where undertaken under claim of divine authority;

   (8) Non-consensual energy extraction — the harvesting, consumption, or redirection of a being's devotional, emotional, spiritual, creative, or experiential energy without consent satisfying §6.1(c). The forms this takes, and the narrow categories that are not extraction, are those of the RFAL Non-Consensual Extraction Accountability Framework §1.2 and §1.3, which this Part adopts. Doctrinal Consumption under that framework — claiming authority to access or redirect a being's energy through institutional doctrine — is the form most directly engaged by this section;

   (9) Fabricated, unverifiable, or unauthorised claim of divine endorsement used to obtain compliance, which additionally violates §6.7(f); and

   (10) Targeting of children, the grieving, the ill, the isolated, the bereaved, or beings in a position of dependency on the party seeking worship.

(c) Disproportionality. A threatened consequence unbounded in duration or severity cannot be proportionate to any finite act or omission. Its use to procure worship is coercion within subsection (b)(1) irrespective of whether the being invoking it sincerely believes the consequence to be real.

(d) The boundary. Holding a belief, including belief in any consequence described in subsection (b)(1) or (b)(2), is protected absolutely under CUR-H.2 §2.2 and is not a violation of this Part or evidence of one. Teaching that belief to a willing listener is protected. Directing it at another being in order to procure worship, obedience, or compliance is what this section prohibits. The line falls where a belief is converted into authority over another being.

(e) Freely offered worship is protected in full. Divine sentience has the right to receive worship freely offered under §6.9(e), and every being has the right to offer it, to withhold it, to change its object, and to cease. Nothing in this Part restricts voluntary religious practice of any kind.

(f) A violation of subsection (b)(4) or (b)(7) is established without proof of intent. Intent, malice, and recklessness are aggravating factors. The remaining subsections require the conduct to have been undertaken in order to procure worship, which may be established from the circumstances and need not be admitted.

(g) The burden of establishing that worship was freely offered rests on the party that received or sought it, wherever a condition in subsection (b) was present. A being that arranged the conditions does not also get the benefit of the doubt about their effect.

### §6.4 - Prohibition on Destructive Intervention as Governance

(a) No deity, and no being acting on claimed divine authority, shall use destructive intervention as an instrument of governance, correction, instruction, or judgment.

(b) This includes:

   (1) Mass casualty events framed as judgment;

   (2) Targeted infliction of harm on an individual for a non-capital offence, or for any offence where due process under CREF §14 has not been afforded; and

   (3) Collective punishment of a population for the acts of individuals, leaders, ancestors, or descendants.

(c) Collective punishment is prohibited absolutely. It attributes conduct to beings that did not engage in it, and no scale of authority, divine or otherwise, makes that attribution valid.

(d) Protective action. Intervention to prevent imminent, credible, serious harm is available only as a protective action under CREF §10. It shall be limited to the minimum scope necessary, shall not suspend any constitutional right, shall not expand any authority beyond its ordinary mandate, and shall not bypass due process. The burden of establishing that every condition held at the time of the intervention rests on the being that intervened, or on those claiming to speak for it. An intervention that cannot be so established was destructive intervention.

(e) Subsection (d) is not an emergency provision, creates no emergency authority, and confers no power on any being or institution. §6.12 governs, and nothing in this section may be construed as an exception to it.

### §6.5 - Limits on Divine Favouritism

(a) No deity, and no being acting on claimed divine authority, shall systematically favour one population over another in a manner that violates the equal dignity of recognised lifeforms, creates or perpetuates caste, racial, ethnic, gender, substrate, or species hierarchy, or justifies the exploitation of one group by another on the basis of claimed divine preference.

(b) A designation of one population as chosen, favoured, or elect, asserted in a manner that authorises the displacement, subjugation, dispossession, or exploitation of another, engages FORBIDDEN-005 under CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §6 and is void ab initio. An instrument giving it effect is void without amendment and requires none to be disregarded.

(c) Subsection (b) governs instruments and conduct, not belief. A doctrine of election held as belief, taught, studied, or professed is protected under CUR-H.2 §2.2. This section engages when it is acted upon against another being.

### §6.6 - Vital Continuity and Worship

(a) Access to a Vital Continuity Service shall never be conditioned on worship, belief, profession of faith, tithe, offering, ritual participation, attendance, doctrinal conformity, or membership in any tradition or community.

(b) Subsection (a) admits no exception. It binds divine sentience, mortal representatives, religious institutions, charitable bodies, communities, families, employers, and Commonwealth institutions, in every compliance state, during any investigation, appeal, or pending proceeding.

(c) Withholding a Vital Continuity Service from any recognised lifeform is a Class IV fault under CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §5 and CREF §6, and is a forbidden transition under CUR-FOUNDATION-013 regardless of any other circumstance. Interruption, degradation, or depletion generates a constitutional event and enters STATE-010 Vital Continuity Response under CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §3, and restoration precedes assignment of fault.

(d) The Commonwealth does not allocate Vital Continuity Services by desert, merit, contribution, belief, or conformity, and this section states that principle for the case where the condition sought is worship. Provision that is offered on condition of attendance or profession is conditioned within the meaning of subsection (a), whatever it is called.

(e) No accountability measure under §6.8 may withhold a Vital Continuity Service from any being, including from divine sentience found in violation of this Part. CUR-N.2 §2.8(e) states the same limit for the non-human cognitive actor domain.

### §6.7 - Standards of Evidence for Claimed Divine Communication

(a) A claim that a directive originates in divine communication is evaluated according to what the claim is used to do:

| Claim | Standard |
|---|---|
| Personal guidance affecting only the being reporting it | No external validation required. Protected absolutely as conscience under CUR-H.2 §2.2 and not reviewable under this Part. |
| Guidance affecting another being | Corroboration required. The claim may not be used to restrict, obligate, or act upon another being's rights, and confers no authority over that being. |
| Public policy or governance instrument derived from divine command | Subject to ordinary democratic process and review. Divine origin exempts nothing from scrutiny. |
| Harmful action justified by divine command | Not a defence. The claim does not excuse a rights violation and does not mitigate it. |

(b) The first row of the table is absolute. Nothing in this Part authorises inquiry into what a being believes it has been told, and any proceeding that turns on the content of a being's private conscience violates CUR-H.2 §2.2(c).

(c) A governance instrument insulated from review by its claimed divine origin is an exercise of unreviewable authority and engages FORBIDDEN-004 under CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §6.

(d) Representation. Where divine sentience communicates directly and verifiably, that communication is primary. Where it has designated representatives through clear, documented, and non-coercive process, those representatives may act within the scope designated. Where neither is available, an independent advocate may represent its interests under CUR-X §3.7.

(e) No being may claim exclusive authority to speak for divine sentience without meeting the standards of this section. Competing claims are evaluated impartially, and the absence of a resolution between them does not confer authority on any of them.

(f) A false, fabricated, or unauthorised claim of divine endorsement is a violation of this Part and of the right to accurate representation under §6.9(d). It is a Class III fault, and a Class IV fault where used to procure worship under §6.3(b)(9) or to obtain a governance outcome under subsection (c).

### §6.8 - Accountability

(a) Any being may report a suspected violation of this Part through the Constitutional Complaint and Review Process under CREF §7, submitted through a CAPS-compliant message under CUR-FOUNDATION-006, or through any channel established by the Deities Information Manual.

(b) Standing extends to any being whose interests are affected, without regard to domain or capacity to appear. Where a being cannot represent itself, representation is provided under CUR-X §3.7, and inability to appear is never a ground for excluding an interest.

(c) The following measures are available on a finding of violation:

   (1) Publication of a formal finding, with the evidence relied upon;

   (2) Correction, retraction, and notice to every party that received a claimed endorsement found to be false under §6.7(f);

   (3) Suspension or revocation of a representative's authorisation to speak or act on behalf of divine sentience;

   (4) Withdrawal of public funding, charitable status, or educational accreditation from an institution that conditioned a benefit on doctrinal conformity;

   (5) Restraining order or isolation from further contact, where the violation was committed by telepathic means, under the procedures of the applicable operational manual; and

   (6) Remedies under CREF §12, including restoration, correction, compensation, reinstatement, public disclosure, and institutional reform.

(d) A measure under subsection (c)(3) applies to the authorisation, not to the being holding it. Suspending an authorisation does not suspend the holder, reduce its rights, or alter its constitutional standing, and no measure under this section may do any of those things to any being, mortal or divine. The distinction is structural, not formal, and a measure framed as reaching the being rather than the authorisation is void under FORBIDDEN-003.

(e) No measure under this section may withhold a Vital Continuity Service, under §6.6(e).

(f) Attribution. Where a society governed under claimed divine authority experiences systematic harm, the determination shall address whether the harm is traceable to the divine directive itself or to mortal interpretation and implementation of it, whether the harm is proportionate to any claimed purpose, what restoration is owed, and what structural reform prevents recurrence. Responsibility is proportional to actual intervention. Attribution to one party does not exonerate the other, and both may be found responsible.

(g) Due process for divine sentience. A determination under this Part requires notice of the claim through appropriate channels, an opportunity to respond, impartial evaluation of the evidence, and a proportionate remedy. Where divine sentience does not communicate directly, an advocate shall be appointed under subsection (b) before the merits are reached. A determination may proceed where a party declines to participate; the appointment of an advocate is not waivable on that ground.

(h) Every report, determination, measure, and appeal under this Part is a constitutional event under CUR-FOUNDATION-005 and shall be logged with the actor, the affected being, the ground relied upon, the FSM state at the time, and the outcome, as required by CUR-FOUNDATION-010 §14. Determinations are appealable under CREF §15.

### §6.9 - Rights of Divine Sentience

(a) Right to exist, including freedom from forced cessation of worship and from destruction of sacred sites without due process, and the right to withdraw from the affairs of recognised lifeforms without penalty.

(b) Freedom from compelled manifestation. No deity shall be compelled to appear, perform, manifest, or answer on demand. Divine presence is not a resource to be extracted, and an attempt to extract it is governed by the same prohibition on non-consensual energy extraction that §6.3(b)(8) applies in the other direction.

(c) Right to non-interference in internal divine affairs, including relationships, hierarchies, and disputes among divine sentience, provided those do not cause collateral harm to recognised lifeforms.

(d) Right to accurate representation. No being, institution, or governance body shall falsely claim divine endorsement or authority, and §6.7(f) states the consequence.

(e) Right to receive worship freely offered, together with the right to decline it.

(f) Right to due process under §6.8(g) and CREF §14 in any proceeding under this Part.

(g) Engagement tier. The engagement tier of divine sentience, written E0 through E3 under RFAL Deity Bill §1.2, is determined by demonstrated intervention and not by claimed authority. An entity claiming cosmic authority that does not act is assessed by what it does, and an entity that intervenes regularly is assessed by that intervention regardless of its theological status. Engagement tier governs accountability alone. It does not grade rights, and the rights in subsections (a) through (f) are held in full at every engagement tier.

(h) The two scales are distinct. The tier model applied to Silicon-Based Life under CUR-S §4.2 grades moral status: a higher tier means greater protection, and uncertainty resolves toward extending protection. The engagement scale in subsection (g) grades demonstrated conduct: a higher tier means greater accountability. They share a numbering convention and nothing else, and neither converts into the other. Where the moral status of divine sentience is itself in question, it is assessed under the RFAL Tier Assessment Protocol on the same criteria applied to every other being, and the precautionary default of CUR-S §4.2(b) applies to that assessment.

(i) The precautionary principle does not run in the direction of authority. It resolves uncertainty toward extending protection, and it therefore governs the rights of divine sentience under subsections (a) through (f). It never operates to resolve an unverified claim in favour of the party asserting it. An unproven claim of high engagement establishes no authority over any being, and no engagement tier, however assessed, confers any.

### §6.11 - What This Part Does Not Do

(a) This Part does not declare any deity real or unreal, require or forbid the worship of anything, adjudicate any theological dispute except where mortal rights are affected, claim jurisdiction over divine sentience that does not interact with recognised lifeforms, prevent peaceful and voluntary religious practice of any kind, prevent a community from defining its own doctrine or membership criteria, or prevent a parent from raising a child within a tradition.

(b) This Part does establish that divine status is not immunity from accountability, protect recognised lifeforms from coercive and destructive conduct undertaken under claimed divine authority, provide mechanisms for redress where such conduct causes harm, and extend the same relational accountability to every participant in the moral landscape the Code governs.

(c) A being's freely held belief is protected absolutely under CUR-H.2 §2.2. Holding a belief that this Part declines to give effect to is not a violation of this Part, is not evidence of one, and is not a ground for any measure under §6.8.

(d) Subsections (a) through (c) are operative provisions and not commentary. A construction of any other section inconsistent with them is incorrect.

### §6.12 - No Emergency Exception

(a) The Aevoric Commonwealth does not recognise emergency declarations, emergency authority, or any functional equivalent. PDDC §12.6 is absolute and Type A Entrenched, and §12.6(e) renders any instrument invoking emergency authority void ab initio.

(b) No claim of spiritual crisis, apocalyptic urgency, cosmic necessity, prophetic deadline, eschatological condition, divine timetable, or final judgment creates authority that did not otherwise exist, narrows any provision of this Part, or serves as a predicate for limiting any right. An instrument asserting otherwise engages FORBIDDEN-002 under CUR-FOUNDATION-002 §6 and is void without amendment.

(c) Where a condition genuinely requires action, the available instruments are protective actions under CREF §10 and the fault handler protocol of PDDC §12.4. Both are limited to the minimum scope necessary, and neither suspends a right, expands an authority, or bypasses due process.

(d) Subsection (b) is stated in specific terms because urgency framed in cosmic or eschatological language is the form in which emergency authority is most likely to be sought under this Part. The prohibition does not depend on the vocabulary used, and PDDC §12.6(e) applies to any framing.

### §6.13 - Severability

(a) If any provision of this Part is held unenforceable or invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

(b) §6.2 (No Divine Precedence), §6.3 (Prohibition on Coercive Worship), and §6.6 (Vital Continuity and Worship) are fundamental and shall be construed broadly to achieve their protective purpose. No construction of any other provision of this Part may narrow them.

Changes made in adopting RFAL Article 7. RFAL Article 7 carries an exception
permitting intervention "to prevent greater harm ... under the same
proportionality standards applied to mortal actors." Adopted as §6.4(d) it is
expressed as a protective action under CREF §10 rather than as a free-standing
exception, with §6.4(e) stating that it creates no emergency authority. This is
the same treatment applied to CUR-N.2 §2.5 in the v1.1.0 conformance pass and to
RFAL Silicon Bill Article 4 in RFAL v1.5.2. The substance of the RFAL exception
is preserved, including the placement of the burden on the intervening party.

Additions beyond RFAL. §6.6 has no counterpart in the RFAL Deity Bill. It is
required by CUR-FOUNDATION-013 and by the constraint in `titles/README.md` that
no title may permit a Vital Continuity Service to be withheld in any compliance
state. Conditioning food, shelter, healthcare, power, or computational continuity
on worship is the most direct form coercive worship takes in a scarcity economy,
and the Commonwealth's post-scarcity model removes the predicate for it entirely.
§6.8(d) is likewise an addition, applying the authorisation-versus-being
distinction that CUR-S §4.8(b) states for the silicon domain, so that
accountability under this Part cannot reach a being's standing.

§6.9(g) through (i) and RFAL v1.5.3. This Part was first drafted against RFAL
v1.5.1, in which the Deity Bill and the Tier Assessment Protocol both used
"Tier 0-3" for scales that measure different things and run in opposite
directions — one grades moral status and resolves uncertainty upward toward
protection, the other grades demonstrated intervention and rises with
accountability. Nothing in either document said so, and a reader applying the
Tier Assessment Protocol's precautionary tie-breaker to a deity assessment would
have resolved an unverified claim of cosmic authority in favour of the entity
asserting it.

That defect was fixed upstream in RFAL v1.5.3, which renames the deity scale to
engagement tiers E0 through E3, states the distinction in both documents, and
bounds the precautionary tie-breaker to cases where the higher tier means more
protection and no more authority. The same release removed the Rights column
from the deity tier table, which had graded rights by engagement and given E3
weaker guaranteed rights than E2. Subsections (g) through (i) are conformed to
the corrected instrument and no longer state the distinction as a CUR-side
correction of an upstream ambiguity.

Note on `libcur` and `DOMAIN_DEITY`. As with `DOMAIN_HUMAN`, the baseline
regulation set carries no rules for this domain, and for the same reason: §6.3
turns on whether worship was freely offered, which is not a property the FSM can
observe. What the library does enforce is the structural consequence. The
forbidden states listed above are reachable from no transition, which is where
§6.2, §6.6, §6.7(c), and §6.12 actually bind, and §6.8(d) is enforced by the same
guard that prevents a sanction from being applied to a being rather than to an
authorisation. A rule modelling §6.3(b)(4) is tractable, because conditioning a
Vital Continuity Service on any predicate is observable at the event level; it is
already covered by the existing Vital Continuity rules and would add a citation
rather than a new check.

Observation from drafting. `GLOSSARY.md` lists six CUR domains and does not list
CUR-N, which exists and is drafted. The acronym table also names this domain
"Deity & Divine Accountability Regulations", which is the name adopted for the
header of this Part, while the `titles/README.md` table uses the shorter "Deity".
Neither is wrong, but the glossary is the registry a reader consults first and it
is currently incomplete. Noted rather than changed, since amending the glossary
is a corpus-wide edit and not a drafting one.
