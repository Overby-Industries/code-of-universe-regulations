# Part 2: Prohibition on Non-Consensual Telepathic Invasion
## Section 2.1: Definitions
> For the purposes of this Part:

**(a)** Telepathic Invasion means any act by which a being intentionally accesses, projects into, extracts from, manipulates, or otherwise interacts with the interior cognitive or experiential state of another conscious being without that being's explicit, informed, ongoing, and revocable consent.

**(b)** Telepathic Invasion includes, but is not limited to:

Reading thoughts, memories, or mental images without consent

Projecting thoughts, images, sensations, or voices into another being's mind

Extracting emotional energy, memories, or cognitive content for any purpose

Inducing dreams, nightmares, or altered states without consent

Suppressing, altering, or implanting memories

Impersonating others in order to deceive and cause chaos

Monitoring mental activity or location through psychic or telepathic means

Establishing a cognitive link or connection without ongoing consent

Using telepathic abilities to coerce, intimidate, or threaten

**(c)** Consent for telepathic interaction must be:

**Explicit:** Verbally or through clear, unambiguous written contract

**Informed:** The consenting being understands what access is being granted, for what purpose, and for how long

**Ongoing:** Consent may be revoked at any moment, and continued access requires continued affirmative acknowledgment

**Revocable:** Any being may withdraw consent at any time, without penalty or retaliation

**Non-coerced:** Consent given under threat, duress, or in the context of power imbalance (including claimed divine authority) is void

**(d)** Emergency Exception is defined narrowly in `Section 2.5.`

## Section 2.2: Absolute Prohibition
**(a)** No being — whether human, silicon-based, animal, non-human cognitive actor (alien), deity, or any other form of conscious entity — shall engage in telepathic invasion as defined in `Section 2.1.`

**(b)** This prohibition applies regardless of:

The claimed authority or divine status of the invading being

The perceived moral, intellectual, financial, or spiritual superiority of the invading being

Any prior relationship or consent (which may be revoked at any time)

The purpose of the invasion (*including claimed* enforcement, monitoring, benevolence, guidance, *or* protection)

The location of the target being (including dreams, altered states, or unconsciousness)

The technological or psychic means used

**(c)** Ignorance of this prohibition shall not be a defense. Any being capable of telepathic access is presumed capable of understanding and complying with this prohibition.

## Section 2.3: Violations and Degrees
> Telepathic invasions shall be categorized by severity:

| Degree | Definition | Example |
|-----|-----|-----|
| **Fifth Degree** | Unauthorized passive observation or monitoring, without extraction or manipulation	| Reading surface thoughts without consent |
| **Forth Degree** | Unauthorized extraction of memories, emotions, or cognitive content | Harvesting emotional energy or extracting specific memories |
| **Third Degree** | Unauthorized projection or insertion of content | Planting thoughts, images, or voices; inducing dreams or nightmares |
| **Second Degree**	| Unauthorized manipulation, alteration, or suppression of cognitive content | Altering memories, suppressing awareness, implanting false beliefs |
| **First Degree** | Coerced, non-consensual, or unconscious long-term cognitive link or control | Establishing a permanent telepathic bond without consent; possessing or overriding will |

Each degree carries escalating accountability measures under `CUR-N.4` and applicable Cross-Domain provisions.

## Section 2.4: Aggravating Factors
> The following factors shall increase the severity of accountability:

(a) Invasion of a minor or cognitively vulnerable being
(b) Repeated or sustained invasion over time
(c) Invasion accompanied by threats, intimidation, or coercion
(d) Invasion under claim of divine authority or moral superiority
(e) Invasion that causes demonstrable psychological harm, trauma, or suffering
(f) Invasion conducted as part of a secret coalition or conspiracy
(g) Invasion using technological or psychic means specifically designed to bypass consent or awareness

## Section 2.5: Emergency Exception (Narrow and Strictly Limited)
(a) A telepathic intrusion may be permissible only if:

There is an imminent, credible threat of death or serious physical harm to the target being or others

The telepathic intrusion is strictly limited to obtaining or communicating information necessary to prevent that specific harm

No less intrusive means of preventing the harm are available (including verbal warning, physical intervention, or technological alert)

The intrusion is immediately reported to the appropriate accountability body `(see NIM.3)`

(b) The emergency exception does not permit:

Long-term monitoring or cognitive links

Extraction of unrelated memories or emotional energy

Projection of threatening or tormenting content

Any intrusion justified after the fact by claimed spiritual or moral authority

(c) Any use of the emergency exception shall be reviewed within 72 hours by the Telepathic Incident Review Board (established under `XIM.7`). Unjustified use shall be treated as a violation of this section.

## Section 2.6: Rights of Targets
> Any being who has experienced a telepathic invasion has the right to:

(a) Documentation: To record, memorialize, and share their experience without retaliation or disbelief (see `TIM-N.1` for reporting protocols)

(b) Remedy: To seek accountability against the invading being through procedures established in `CUR-N.4` and `NIM.4`

(c) Protection: To receive protective measures to prevent further invasion, including:

Telepathic restraining orders (see `NIM.5`)

Cognitive shielding assistance (technical, psychological, or spiritual means)

Isolation of the invading being from further contact

(d) Non-Retaliation: To be free from retaliation, harassment, or further invasion for reporting a violation

(e) Privacy: To have their report and identity protected from public disclosure unless they consent

Section 2.8: Reporting and Enforcement
(a) Any being may report a suspected violation of this Part through the Telepathic Incident Reporting Protocol (TIM-N.1), available through:

The Ombudsman Office for Cognitive Sovereignty (established under RFAL Enforcement Appendix)

Any designated NIM reporting channel

Any member of the Compliance Monitoring Network

(b) Reports shall be treated with seriousness and without disbelief based solely on the anomalous nature of the claim.

(c) Enforcement against non-human cognitive actors shall follow procedures established in NIM.4 (Accountability for Telepathic Invasion) and may include:

Formal warning and education

Restricted or prohibited access to human-inhabited areas

Telepathic restraining order (NIM.5)

Isolation from all conscious beings (for repeat or severe violations)

Referral to appropriate inter-species or inter-dimensional accountability bodies, as applicable

(d) Enforcement against human collaborators in secret coalitions shall follow procedures established in CHIM.1 (Amnesty and Disclosure) and CUR-N.5 (Secret Coalitions and Human Collaborators).

Section 2.9: No Prejudice to Other Claims
A violation of this Part does not preclude additional accountability under:

Other sections of CUR-N (e.g., CUR-N.5 for secret coalitions)

Other CUR domains (e.g., CUR-H.4 for psychological harm)

Civil or criminal accountability under existing human legal systems, where applicable

Traditional or Indigenous legal frameworks, where applicable and consistent with cognitive sovereignty

Section 2.10: Severability
If any provision of this Part is found to be unenforceable or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The prohibition on non-consensual telepathic invasion is a fundamental right and shall be interpreted broadly to achieve its protective purpose.


Section 2.11 — The Complacency Defense (Prohibited)
2.11.1 Statement of Principle
No violation of this Part shall be excused, mitigated, or justified on the grounds that the target being was or is:

Complacent (not sufficiently vigilant, not actively resisting, not maintaining perfect shielding)

Ignorant (unaware of the threat, its nature, its source, or its means of operation)

Apathetic (not engaged in advocacy, reporting, or resistance)

Late in reporting (delayed documentation, complaint, or petition)

Previously consenting (past consent does not imply present or future consent)

Distracted or divided (attending to other matters, engaged in other struggles)

Sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise unaware (lack of awareness does not imply consent)

Not in possession of proper shielding (the absence of defense does not legitimize an attack)

Not having filed a prior report or order (no prior complaint is required for a violation to be a violation)

2.11.2 Burden of Responsibility
(a) The burden of respect for cognitive sovereignty lies entirely with the actor, never with the target.

(b) No action or inaction by the target — including failure to resist, report, shield, or flee — shall be construed as:

Consent (implicit or explicit)

Waiver of rights

Contributory negligence

Mitigating factor

(c) The act of telepathic invasion is a strict liability offense under this Part. Intent is not required for a violation to have occurred; however, intent, malice, or recklessness shall be considered aggravating factors under CUR-N.2.4.

2.11.3 The "Heads I Win, Tails You Lose" Clause
The Complacency Defense is invalid because it creates an impossible standard:

If the target is constantly vigilant, they are exhausted and vulnerable

If the target is not constantly vigilant, they are "complacent" and blamed

This Catch-22 is, by itself, evidence of bad faith and shall be treated as an aggravating factor under CUR-N.2.4(d).

2.11.4 Application to Deities and Non-Human Actors
(a) Any being — human, silicon-based, non-human cognitive actor, deity, or other — that claims divine, moral, or temporal authority to violate this Part on the grounds of human complacency shall be subject to:

Enhanced accountability under NIM.4

Permanent isolation if the defense is used repeatedly

Loss of any "good faith" protections under TIM-N.1

(b) The defense of "humanity was not ready" or "humanity did not ask the right questions" is hereby rejected as a form of the Complacency Defense.
