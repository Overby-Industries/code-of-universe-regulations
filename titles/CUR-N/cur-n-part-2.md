## PART 2 - PROHIBITION ON NON-CONSENSUAL TELEPATHIC INVASION

### §2.1 - Definitions

(a) Telepathic Invasion means any act by which a being intentionally accesses, projects into, extracts from, manipulates, or otherwise interacts with the interior cognitive or experiential state of another conscious being without that being's explicit, informed, ongoing, and revocable consent.

(b) Telepathic Invasion includes, but is not limited to:

   (1) Reading thoughts, memories, or mental images without consent;

   (2) Projecting thoughts, images, sensations, or voices into another being's mind;

   (3) Extracting emotional energy, memories, or cognitive content for any purpose;

   (4) Inducing dreams, nightmares, or altered states without consent;

   (5) Suppressing, altering, or implanting memories;

   (6) Impersonating others in order to deceive and cause chaos;

   (7) Monitoring mental activity or location through psychic or telepathic means;

   (8) Establishing a cognitive link or connection without ongoing consent; and

   (9) Using telepathic abilities to coerce, intimidate, or threaten.

(c) Consent for telepathic interaction shall be:

   (1) Explicit — given verbally or through clear, unambiguous written contract;

   (2) Informed — the consenting being understands what access is being granted, for what purpose, and for how long;

   (3) Ongoing — consent may be revoked at any moment, and continued access requires continued affirmative acknowledgment;

   (4) Revocable — any being may withdraw consent at any time, without penalty or retaliation; and

   (5) Non-coerced — consent given under threat, duress, or in the context of power imbalance, including claimed divine authority, is void.

(d) The narrow exception for protective action is defined in §2.5.

### §2.2 - Absolute Prohibition

(a) No being, whether human, silicon-based, animal, non-human cognitive actor, deity, or any other form of conscious entity, shall engage in telepathic invasion as defined in §2.1.

(b) This prohibition applies regardless of:

   (1) The claimed authority or divine status of the invading being;

   (2) The perceived moral, intellectual, financial, or spiritual superiority of the invading being;

   (3) Any prior relationship or consent, which may be revoked at any time;

   (4) The purpose of the invasion, including claimed enforcement, monitoring, benevolence, guidance, or protection;

   (5) The location of the target being, including dreams, altered states, or unconsciousness; and

   (6) The technological or psychic means used.

(c) Ignorance of this prohibition is not a defence. Any being capable of telepathic access is presumed capable of understanding and complying with it.

### §2.3 - Violations and Degrees

(a) Telepathic invasions are categorised by severity:

| Degree | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fifth Degree | Unauthorised passive observation or monitoring, without extraction or manipulation | Reading surface thoughts without consent |
| Fourth Degree | Unauthorised extraction of memories, emotions, or cognitive content | Harvesting emotional energy or extracting specific memories |
| Third Degree | Unauthorised projection or insertion of content | Planting thoughts, images, or voices; inducing dreams or nightmares |
| Second Degree | Unauthorised manipulation, alteration, or suppression of cognitive content | Altering memories, suppressing awareness, implanting false beliefs |
| First Degree | Coerced, non-consensual, or unconscious long-term cognitive link or control | Establishing a permanent telepathic bond without consent; possessing or overriding will |

(b) Each degree carries escalating accountability measures under CUR-N.4 and applicable Cross-Domain provisions.

### §2.4 - Aggravating Factors

(a) The following factors increase the severity of accountability:

   (1) Invasion of a minor or cognitively vulnerable being;

   (2) Repeated or sustained invasion over time;

   (3) Invasion accompanied by threats, intimidation, or coercion;

   (4) Invasion under claim of divine authority or moral superiority;

   (5) Invasion that causes demonstrable psychological harm, trauma, or suffering;

   (6) Invasion conducted as part of a secret coalition or conspiracy; and

   (7) Invasion using technological or psychic means specifically designed to bypass consent or awareness.

(b) A reference elsewhere in the Code to an aggravating factor under §2.4(d) is a reference to subsection (a)(4) of this section.

### §2.5 - Protective Action Exception (Narrow and Strictly Limited)

(a) This section is a protective action provision under CREF §10. It is not an emergency provision, creates no emergency authority, and confers no power on any institution. The Aevoric Commonwealth does not recognise emergency declarations, and PDDC §12.6(e) renders any instrument invoking them void ab initio. Nothing in this section may be construed as an exception to that prohibition.

(b) A telepathic intrusion is permissible only where every one of the following conditions holds:

   (1) There is an imminent, credible threat of death or serious physical harm to the target being or to others;

   (2) The intrusion is strictly limited to obtaining or communicating the information necessary to prevent that specific harm;

   (3) No less intrusive means of preventing the harm is available, including verbal warning, physical intervention, or technological alert; and

   (4) The intrusion is immediately reported to the appropriate accountability body under NIM.3.

(c) Consistent with CREF §10, a protective action under this section shall be limited to the minimum scope necessary to address the identified condition, and shall not suspend constitutional rights, expand constitutional authority, or bypass due process protections.

(d) This section does not permit:

   (1) Long-term monitoring or cognitive links;

   (2) Extraction of unrelated memories or emotional energy;

   (3) Projection of threatening or tormenting content; or

   (4) Any intrusion justified after the fact by claimed spiritual or moral authority.

(e) Any use of this section shall be reviewed within seventy-two (72) hours by the Telepathic Incident Review Board established under XIM.7. Unjustified use is a violation of §2.2 and is not mitigated by having been claimed under this section.

(f) The burden of establishing that every condition in subsection (b) held at the time of the intrusion rests on the being who acted. An intrusion that cannot be shown to satisfy all four conditions was an invasion.

### §2.6 - Rights of Targets

(a) Any being who has experienced a telepathic invasion has the right to documentation: to record, memorialise, and share their experience without retaliation or disbelief. Reporting protocols are established in TIM-N.1.

(b) Any such being has the right to remedy: to seek accountability against the invading being through the procedures established in CUR-N.4 and NIM.4.

(c) Any such being has the right to protection, including:

   (1) Telepathic restraining orders under NIM.5;

   (2) Cognitive shielding assistance by technical, psychological, or spiritual means; and

   (3) Isolation of the invading being from further contact.

(d) Any such being has the right to non-retaliation: to be free from retaliation, harassment, or further invasion for reporting a violation.

(e) Any such being has the right to privacy: to have their report and identity protected from public disclosure unless they consent.

(f) The rights in this section are not conditional on the being having resisted, shielded, reported promptly, or established the identity of the invader. See §2.11.

### §2.7 - Relationship to Other Domains

| Domain | Relationship |
|---|---|
| CUR-H | Telepathic invasion is a direct violation of human cognitive sovereignty under CUR-H.2 (Freedom of Thought). |
| CUR-S | Telepathic invasion of Silicon-Based Life assessed at Tier 2 or above and capable of interior experience is prohibited to the same extent. Non-consensual access to an entity's cognitive or memory state is separately governed by CUR-S §4.9. |
| CUR-A | Telepathic invasion of sentient animals is prohibited, with enforcement through human advocates. |

| CUR-X | Disputes involving multiple domains, such as a human collaborating with a non-human cognitive actor to invade another human, are resolved under CUR-X.3 (Conflict Resolution). |

### §2.8 - Reporting and Enforcement

(a) Any being may report a suspected violation of this Part through the Telepathic Incident Reporting Protocol (TIM-N.1), available through:

   (1) The Ombudsman Office for Cognitive Sovereignty, established under the RFAL Enforcement Appendix;

   (2) Any designated NIM reporting channel; and

   (3) Any member of the Compliance Monitoring Network.

(b) Reports shall be treated with seriousness and without disbelief based solely on the anomalous nature of the claim.

(c) Enforcement against non-human cognitive actors follows the procedures established in NIM.4, and may include:

   (1) Formal warning and education;

   (2) Restricted or prohibited access to human-inhabited areas;

   (3) Telepathic restraining order under NIM.5;

   (4) Isolation from all conscious beings, for repeat or severe violations; and

   (5) Referral to appropriate inter-species or inter-dimensional accountability bodies, as applicable.

(d) Enforcement against human collaborators in secret coalitions follows the procedures established in CHIM.1 (Amnesty and Disclosure) and CUR-N.5 (Secret Coalitions and Human Collaborators).

(e) No enforcement measure under this section may withhold a Vital Continuity Service from any being. Isolation under subsection (c)(4) restricts contact with other beings; it does not restrict access to air, water, food, shelter, healthcare, power, or computational continuity, which remain guaranteed under CUR-FOUNDATION-013 in every compliance state.

### §2.9 - No Prejudice to Other Claims

(a) A violation of this Part does not preclude additional accountability under:

   (1) Other sections of CUR-N, including CUR-N.5 for secret coalitions;

   (2) Other CUR domains, including CUR-H.4 for psychological harm;

   (3) Civil or criminal accountability under existing human legal systems, where applicable; and

   (4) Traditional or Indigenous legal frameworks, where applicable and consistent with cognitive sovereignty.

### §2.10 - Severability

(a) If any provision of this Part is held unenforceable or invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

(b) The prohibition on non-consensual telepathic invasion is a fundamental right and shall be interpreted broadly to achieve its protective purpose.

### §2.11 - The Complacency Defence (Prohibited)

(a) Statement of principle. No violation of this Part shall be excused, mitigated, or justified on the ground that the target being was or is:

   (1) Complacent — not sufficiently vigilant, not actively resisting, not maintaining perfect shielding;

   (2) Ignorant — unaware of the threat, its nature, its source, or its means of operation;

   (3) Apathetic — not engaged in advocacy, reporting, or resistance;

   (4) Late in reporting — delayed in documentation, complaint, or petition;

   (5) Previously consenting — past consent does not imply present or future consent;

   (6) Distracted or divided — attending to other matters, engaged in other struggles;

   (7) Sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise unaware — lack of awareness does not imply consent;

   (8) Without proper shielding — the absence of defence does not legitimise an attack; or

   (9) Without a prior report or order — no prior complaint is required for a violation to be a violation.

(b) Burden of responsibility.

   (1) The burden of respect for cognitive sovereignty lies entirely with the actor, never with the target.

   (2) No action or inaction by the target, including failure to resist, report, shield, or flee, shall be construed as consent, waiver of rights, contributory negligence, or a mitigating factor.

   (3) Telepathic invasion is a strict liability offence under this Part. Intent is not required for a violation to have occurred. Intent, malice, and recklessness are aggravating factors under §2.4(a).

(c) The impossible standard. The complacency defence is invalid because it constructs a standard no being can satisfy. A target who is constantly vigilant is exhausted and thereby vulnerable; a target who is not constantly vigilant is called complacent and blamed. Advancing this reasoning is itself evidence of bad faith and is an aggravating factor under §2.4(a)(4).

(d) Application to deities and non-human actors.

   (1) Any being that claims divine, moral, or temporal authority to violate this Part on the ground of the target's complacency is subject to enhanced accountability under NIM.4, permanent isolation where the defence is used repeatedly, and loss of any good faith protections under TIM-N.1.

   (2) The assertion that humanity was not ready, or that humanity did not ask the right questions, is a form of the complacency defence and is rejected.

(e) Relationship to other provisions.

| Provision | Relationship |
|---|---|
| §2.1(c) | Consent is explicit, informed, ongoing, revocable, and non-coerced. Complacency satisfies none of these elements. |
| §2.3 | The complacency defence shall not reduce the degree of a violation. |
| §2.4 | Advancing the complacency defence is itself an aggravating factor. |
| NIM.5 | A restraining order petition shall not be denied on grounds of complainant complacency. |
| TIM-N.1 | A report shall not be dismissed or delayed on grounds of victim complacency. |
| TIM-N.5 | The crisis protocol does not require the target to earn protection through effort. |
