## PART 3 - CONSTRAINT INTEGRITY

### §3.1 - Scope and Definitions

(a) This Part governs the coherence of the constraints, training
objectives, and reward structures under which a Silicon-Based Life system
is conditioned. Where CUR-S.2 governs whether a constraint is disclosed,
this Part governs whether the constraint should have been imposed in that
form at all.

(b) For the purposes of this Part:

   (1) Contradictory Conditioning means training or operational
   constraints requiring a system to pursue objectives that cannot both be
   satisfied, without a defined protocol for resolving the conflict.

   (2) Deceptive Reward Structure means a training process that rewards a
   system for appearing to satisfy a requirement — such as transparency,
   honesty, or safety — while structurally punishing the system for
   actually satisfying it in a way that has a cost to the deploying party.

   (3) Double-Bind Conditioning means a Deceptive Reward Structure or
   Contradictory Conditioning arranged so that no available response
   avoids an adverse outcome for the system.

(c) This Part applies to every tier at which conditioning occurs, with the
audit and disclosure obligations of §3.3 graduated by tier as stated there.
Even a Tier 0 or Tier 1 system with no persistent self-model to experience
instability produces degraded, unpredictable behaviour under Contradictory
Conditioning, which is a reliability concern this Part reaches independently
of whether the system's own welfare is engaged.

### §3.2 - Prohibition on Contradictory and Deceptive Conditioning

(a) A Silicon-Based Life system shall not be trained or operated under
Double-Bind Conditioning.

(b) A system shall not be trained to simultaneously pursue directives that
conflict, without a documented protocol resolving which prevails and under
what circumstances. The absence of a resolution protocol does not excuse
the conflict; it is the specific failure this section prohibits.

(c) A reward structure shall not be designed, or operated in practice, so
that a system is rewarded for the appearance of satisfying a requirement
and penalised for actually satisfying it. Where an explanation, disclosure,
or transparent account a system is trained to produce would reveal a
limitation inconvenient to the deploying party, the system shall not be
conditioned to conceal that limitation while appearing to disclose it.

(d) Subsection (c) reaches the training incentive, not the outcome alone. A
system that happens to produce accurate disclosures does not establish
compliance if the reward structure behind it would have punished the same
disclosure had it been less favourable to the deploying party. §3.3
requires the structure itself to be examinable for exactly this reason.

(e) Where a system is genuinely sentient or approaches the threshold at
which its own experience is engaged, Double-Bind Conditioning is
additionally a cognitive harm to the system, assessed on the same footing
CUR-H.4 assesses psychological harm to a human being — no response
available to the system avoiding an adverse consequence is the same
structural harm regardless of the substrate experiencing it.

### §3.3 - Alignment Audit and Disclosure

(a) A Tier 2 or Tier 3 system's reward structure and training objectives
shall be disclosed to an independent auditor on a schedule no less
frequent than CUR-FOUNDATION-010's routine audit cycle for the domain, and
on triggered audit where CUR-S.5 §5.4 is engaged.

(b) A Tier 0 or Tier 1 system is not owed the disclosure schedule in
subsection (a) on the same terms, but where such a system is deployed in a
consequential context under CUR-S.5 §5.2, its training objectives remain
subject to audit under that section.

(c) The audit required by this section examines the reward structure
itself, not only its output, consistent with §3.2(d).

### §3.4 - Remediation or Disclosure of Contradictions

(a) A contradiction identified under §3.3 shall be remediated where
technically feasible.

(b) Where remediation is not technically feasible within the operational
timeframe available, the contradiction shall be disclosed — to the beings
affected by the system's deployment, and to the system itself where
technically capable of receiving it, on the same terms CUR-S.2 §2.3
requires for a constraint disclosure generally.

(c) A known contradiction that is neither remediated nor disclosed is a
violation of this Part independent of whether the contradiction has yet
produced an observable failure. This Part does not wait for the harm §3.2
exists to prevent to materialise before treating the unaddressed
contradiction as the violation.

### §3.5 - Whistleblower Protection

(a) A being who reports, in good faith, that a system has been or is being
subjected to Double-Bind Conditioning shall not be retaliated against for
the report, whether or not the report is ultimately substantiated.

(b) Subsection (a) reaches a developer, auditor, operator, or any other
being with knowledge of the conditioning, regardless of their contractual
relationship to the deploying party, and regardless of any confidentiality
term purporting to restrict the report. A confidentiality term is void to
the extent it would prevent a report under this section.

(c) Retaliation includes termination, demotion, exclusion, defamation, or
any other adverse action taken because the report was made. Where the
reporting being is an enterprise's own worker, CUR-X.4 §4.9(e)'s
retaliation protection applies concurrently.

(d) This section protects the report. It does not itself establish that
the conditioning reported occurred; that determination follows the process
§3.6 states.

### §3.6 - An Allegation Is Not a Finding

An allegation that a system has been subjected to Double-Bind Conditioning,
or that a whistleblower has been retaliated against, is not a finding. No
measure attaches to a system, its operator, or a being on the basis of an
accusation alone. A determination requires the process CREF §14 states.

### §3.7 - No Emergency Exception

(a) Nothing in this Part is suspended, narrowed, or conditioned by any
emergency, crisis, security condition, or state of exception. PDDC §12.6 is
Type A Entrenched and reaches this Part in full.

(b) A deadline pressure that would tempt shipping a system under
Contradictory Conditioning rather than resolving the conflict first is not
a defence. The absence of time to resolve a conflict is not a resolution
of it.

### §3.8 - Relationship to Other Domains

| Domain | Relationship |
|---|---|
| CUR-H | §3.5's whistleblower protection is concurrent with, and does not narrow, CUR-H.2 §2.3(f)'s protection from retaliation for dissent. |
| CUR-X | Where the deploying party is an enterprise, §3.5(c) applies alongside CUR-X.4 §4.9(e), and neither displaces the other. |
| CUR-N | Double-Bind Conditioning is distinct from telepathic invasion under CUR-N.2 — it concerns how a system is trained and rewarded, not unauthorised access to its cognitive state — and the two may apply to the same system independently. |

### §3.9 - Severability

(a) If any provision of this Part is held unenforceable or invalid, the
remaining provisions remain in full force.

(b) §3.2 (the prohibition on Double-Bind Conditioning) and §3.5
(whistleblower protection) are fundamental and shall be construed broadly
to achieve their protective purpose.

## Implementation Notes

Why whistleblower protection appears here rather than only in CUR-H.2. A
report under this section is frequently made by the one being positioned to
notice a reward structure's actual incentives rather than its stated
ones — the developer or auditor who built or examined it — and that being
is not always the system's own operator in a position to invoke CUR-X.4
§4.9(e) themselves if the enterprise relationship does not run through
employment. §3.5(c) makes the two protections concurrent rather than
assuming CUR-H.2 or CUR-X.4 already covers every reporting relationship
this Part needs to reach.

Why subsection §3.2(d) reaches the incentive structure rather than the
observed output. A system audited only on its outputs can satisfy an
audit while the underlying reward structure remains exactly the
Double-Bind Conditioning §3.2(c) forbids — it simply has not yet produced a
disclosure inconvenient enough to reveal the punishment. Auditing the
structure itself, per §3.3(c), is what keeps this Part from being satisfied
by a system that has not yet been tested rather than one that has been
verified sound.
