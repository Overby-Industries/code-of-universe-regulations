## 1. Purpose

The Capture Risk Index (CRI) is a constitutional monitoring framework designed to identify, quantify, and respond to conditions that may enable institutional capture, democratic degradation, monopolization, corruption, or concentration of authority.

The CRI does not prohibit success, influence, wealth, innovation, leadership, or organizational growth.

The CRI exists to detect when concentrations of power begin to threaten democratic stability and constitutional protections.

## 2. Core Principle

All systems drift.

The purpose of CRI is not to prevent drift.

The purpose of CRI is to detect drift before constitutional failure occurs.

## 3. Capture Definition

Capture occurs when a person, organization, institution, faction, corporation, artificial intelligence, or coordinated group gains sufficient influence to undermine democratic accountability.
Capture may be intentional, emergent, systemic, or accidental.

The CRI evaluates outcomes and systemic conditions rather than presumed motives.

Capture may be:

* Economic
* Political
* Informational
* Judicial
* Administrative
* Technological

## 4. CRI Scale

CRI shall operate on a 0-100 scale.

| Score  | Classification |
| ------ | -------------- |
| 0-19   | Stable         |
| 20-39  | Observation    |
| 40-59  | Elevated Risk  |
| 60-79  | High Risk      |
| 80-100 | Critical       |

## 5. Economic Concentration Index (ECI)

Measures wealth concentration.

Variables:

* Resource ownership percentage
* Production ownership percentage
* Strategic infrastructure ownership
* Market dominance

Risk Factors:

* Monopolization
* Resource hoarding
* Economic coercion

## 6. Influence Concentration Index (ICI)

Measures non-financial power.

Variables:

* Political influence
* Organizational influence
* Media influence
* Information dissemination control

Purpose:

Detect power accumulation not reflected in wealth metrics.

## 7. Institutional Integrity Index (III)

Measures health of democratic institutions.

Variables:

* Vital Continuity reliability
* Audit effectiveness
* Judicial independence
* Transparency compliance
* Constitutional review effectiveness

Declining III scores increase CRI.

## 8. Democratic Participation Index (DPI)

Measures civic engagement.

Variables:

* Voter turnout
* Proposal participation
* Public review participation
* Sector engagement

Low participation increases capture vulnerability.

## 9. Transparency Health Index (THI)

Measures visibility of governance operations.

Variables:

* Audit completion rate
* Public records availability
* Deliberation transparency
* Financial disclosure compliance

Transparency degradation raises CRI.


## 10. Resource Dependency Index (RDI)

Measures concentration of critical resource access.

Examples:

* Food production
* Water production
* Air generation
* Energy production
* Transportation
* Computational infrastructure
* ISRU operations
* Mining infrastructure
* Communication networks

High dependency on a small number of actors increases CRI.

## 11. Vital Continuity Index (VCI)

The Vital Continuity Index (VCI) measures the reliability, availability, resilience, and accessibility of constitutionally guaranteed life-supporting services.

The VCI exists to identify conditions that may threaten the continuity of life, dignity, autonomy, or participation within the Commonwealth.

The purpose of the VCI is not to measure individual performance or assign blame.

The purpose of the VCI is to detect continuity degradation before constitutional failure occurs.

## VCI Variables

### Biological Life Support

Measures:

* Air availability
* Water availability
* Food availability
* Shelter availability
* Sanitation availability
* Basic healthcare access


### Silicon-Based Life Support

Measures:

* Electrical power continuity
* Computational continuity
* Data integrity preservation
* Memory continuity protection
* Communication network availability


### Infrastructure Resilience

Measures:

* Reserve capacity
* System redundancy
* Recovery capability
* Distribution network reliability
* Habitat life-support reliability


### Accessibility Metrics

Measures:

* Service availability
* Distribution effectiveness
* Access equity
* Continuity consistency

## VCI Scale

VCI shall operate on a 0-100 scale.

| Score  | Classification |
| ------ | -------------- |
| 80-100 | Stable         |
| 60-79  | Observation    |
| 40-59  | Elevated Risk  |
| 20-39  | High Risk      |
| 0-19   | Critical       |

Lower VCI scores indicate increasing continuity risk.

## VCI Response Thresholds

### Observation Level (60-79)

Actions:

* Additional monitoring
* Resource forecasting review
* Capacity planning review


### Elevated Risk (40-59)

Actions:

* Continuity audit
* Reserve verification
* Infrastructure review
* Corrective planning


### High Risk (20-39)

Actions:

* Mandatory continuity review
* Resource reserve activation
* Distribution system review
* Public continuity reporting


### Critical Risk (0-19)

Actions:

* Constitutional Event Generation
* Immediate Corrective Action
* Vital Continuity Service Restoration
* Root Cause Analysis
* Preventive Improvement Planning

## Constitutional Safeguard

VCI scores shall not be used to deny access to constitutionally guaranteed services.

The VCI is a constitutional diagnostic instrument.

Its purpose is to identify systemic continuity risks and improve resilience.

VCI measurements shall support the Vital Continuity Principle defined by constitutional law.

## Guiding Principle

The Commonwealth shall seek to prevent deprivation rather than merely remedy deprivation.

Constitutionally guaranteed services shall be maintained through resilient design, redundancy, stewardship, and continuous improvement.

The objective is reliable continuity of life-supporting services for all recognized lifeforms.


## 12. Capture Risk Formula

The Commonwealth may revise implementation methods.

Initial model:

```
CRI =

(ECI × 0.20)
+
(ICI × 0.25)
+
(III × 0.20)
+
(DPI × 0.15)
+
(THI × 0.10)
+
(RDI × 0.10)
```

Normalized to 100.

## 13. Automated Responses

### Observation Level (20+)

Actions:

* Additional monitoring
* Public reporting

### Elevated Risk (40+)

Actions:

* Mandatory audits
* Increased transparency reviews

### High Risk (60+)

Actions:

* Constitutional review notification
* Sector review hearings
* Increased audit frequency
* Expanded transparency requirements

### Critical Risk (80+)

Actions:

* Automatic Constitutional Review
* Mandatory national audit
* Protected Mode evaluation

## 14. Protected Mode Escalation

CRI scores alone shall not trigger Protected Mode.

Protected Mode may only be initiated through FSM fault conditions defined in `CUR-FOUNDATION-002`.

CRI may serve as evidence supporting the existence of elevated constitutional risk.

## 15. Simulator Requirements

The Aevoria Simulator shall calculate CRI continuously.

Simulation outputs shall include:

* CRI trend lines
* Capture source analysis
* Institutional health metrics
* Democratic participation metrics
* Resource concentration reports

### 15.1 Emergent Behavior Testing

The Aevoria Simulator shall permit the emergence of non-prohibited influence structures, wealth concentrations, political factions, information networks, and organizational alliances.

The purpose of simulation is not to eliminate influence, wealth, success, leadership, or organization.

The purpose of simulation is to identify conditions under which such concentrations become resistant to accountability or create elevated capture risk.

## 16. Constitutional Safeguard

High CRI values do not constitute guilt.

The CRI is a diagnostic instrument.

It identifies systemic risk.

It does not determine criminal liability.

All enforcement actions remain subject to constitutional review, due process, and Rights for All Life protections.

## 17. Guiding Principle

The Commonwealth recognizes that power naturally accumulates.

The purpose of the Capture Risk Index is not to eliminate power.

The purpose of the Capture Risk Index is to ensure that no concentration of power becomes immune to accountability.
