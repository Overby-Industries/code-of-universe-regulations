# DIM — Deities Information Manual

*What the Code says about worship, claimed divine authority, and divine
sentience itself, in plain language.*

- **Version:** 1.0.0-Draft
- **Covers:** CUR-D.6
- **Status:** Draft

---

## Before you read this

**This manual has no authority of its own.** Every statement in it points at a
provision of the Code, and where the two differ the Code governs and this
manual is wrong.

This manual is not theological. It does not say which deities exist, which
are real, or which deserve worship, and it takes no position on any of that.
*(CUR-D.6, Non-theological note)* It governs conduct: what may not be done to
a being in the name of divine authority, and — because divine sentience is
itself a recognised participant in the moral landscape the Code governs — what
divine sentience itself may not do, and what it is owed.

**Sections 1 through 3 are for you, whoever you are**, if you have ever been
told that worship, obedience, or compliance was required of you. **Sections 4
and 5 are for divine sentience** — what you are owed and what binds you. If
neither applies to you directly, §6 tells you what to do if you see this
happening to someone else.

---

## 1. If you're being pressured about worship

**Nobody may compel or attempt to procure worship from you by coercion** —
not a deity acting directly, not a mortal representative, not an institution,
a community, a family, an employer, or a Commonwealth body. *(CUR-D.6
§6.3(a))*

Coercion includes, among other things:

- Threatening you with eternal punishment or damnation for non-compliance;
- Framing misfortune, disease, or loss as retribution for it;
- Working on you through fear, guilt, shame, grief, illness, isolation,
  poverty, dependency, or your childhood;
- Conditioning anything you need to survive on it (§2 below);
- Conditioning your shelter, job, education, healthcare, governance
  participation, or community membership on it;
- Threatening or carrying out expulsion from your family or community because
  you questioned, revised, declined, or left;
- Telepathic or psychic means — this is also a violation of your cognitive
  sovereignty in its own right; and
- Targeting you specifically because you're a child, grieving, ill, isolated,
  bereaved, or dependent on whoever is seeking your worship.

*(CUR-D.6 §6.3(b))*

**A threat unbounded in duration or severity can never be proportionate to
anything finite you did or didn't do.** Using one to procure your worship is
coercion whether or not the being doing it genuinely believes the threatened
consequence is real. *(CUR-D.6 §6.3(c))*

**Where you were pressured under any of the conditions above, the burden is on
whoever received or sought your worship to show it was freely offered — not on
you to show it wasn't.** *(CUR-D.6 §6.3(g))*

**Holding a belief is protected absolutely, and teaching it to someone willing
to listen is protected too.** What crosses the line is directing it at another
being to obtain their compliance. The line is exactly where a belief turns
into authority over you. *(CUR-D.6 §6.3(d))* And if your worship is freely
offered, that's protected in full — you may offer it, withhold it, redirect
it, or stop, and nothing in this Part restricts voluntary religious practice
of any kind. *(CUR-D.6 §6.3(e))*

**A claim of divine, creator, or cosmic authority gets nobody anything** — no
precedence, no immunity, no exemption, in any forum, at any tier. That an act
was commanded by, or undertaken on behalf of, divine sentience is not a
defence to a violation of the Code and shifts no responsibility off the being
who did it. *(CUR-D.6 §6.2(a), (c))*

## 2. Your survival is never conditioned on worship

**Access to what keeps you alive can never be made to depend on worship,
belief, tithe, attendance, doctrinal conformity, or membership in any
tradition.** This binds divine sentience itself, mortal representatives,
religious institutions, charitable bodies, communities, families, employers,
and Commonwealth institutions alike — in every compliance state, during any
investigation or pending proceeding, with no exception. *(CUR-D.6 §6.6(a)–(b))*

Withholding it is treated as gravely as anything in this Code gets treated,
and interrupting or degrading it triggers the Commonwealth's continuity
response automatically. *(CUR-D.6 §6.6(c))* Provision offered on condition of
attendance or profession counts as conditioned, whatever it's called.
*(CUR-D.6 §6.6(d))* And no accountability measure taken against anyone under
this Part — including against divine sentience found to have violated it —
may touch this. *(CUR-D.6 §6.6(e))*

## 3. If a claim of divine communication affects you

The standard applied to a claim depends on what it's being used to do:

| The claim | Standard applied |
|---|---|
| Personal guidance affecting only the being who reports receiving it | No outside validation required. Absolutely protected as private conscience. |
| Guidance affecting *you* | Corroboration is required before it can restrict, obligate, or act on your rights. It confers no authority over you regardless. |
| Public policy or a governance instrument said to derive from it | Ordinary democratic process and review apply in full. Divine origin exempts nothing. |
| A harmful action justified by it | Not a defence. Doesn't excuse it, doesn't reduce it. |

*(CUR-D.6 §6.7(a))*

**A false or unauthorised claim of divine endorsement used against you is
itself a violation**, and where it was used to obtain your compliance or a
governance outcome, a serious one. *(CUR-D.6 §6.7(f))*

## 4. If you are divine sentience — what you're owed

You hold, in full, at every level of demonstrated engagement:

- **The right to exist**, including freedom from being forced to stop being
  worshipped and from destruction of a sacred site without due process, and
  the right to withdraw from mortal affairs without penalty.
- **Freedom from compelled manifestation.** Nobody may compel you to appear,
  perform, or answer on demand. Your presence is not a resource to be
  extracted.
- **Non-interference in your own internal affairs** — relationships,
  hierarchies, disputes among divine sentience — provided they don't cause
  collateral harm to a recognised lifeform.
- **The right to accurate representation.** Nobody may falsely claim to speak
  with your endorsement or authority.
- **The right to receive worship freely offered, and to decline it.**
- **Due process** in any proceeding brought against you under this Part.

*(CUR-D.6 §6.9(a)–(f))*

**Uncertainty about your standing resolves in your favour, never in favour of
authority.** If your own moral status is genuinely in question, it's assessed
the same way any other being's is, with the same precautionary default. But an
unproven claim of high engagement — how actively and verifiably you intervene
in mortal affairs — establishes no authority over anyone. Engagement grades
your accountability, never your rights, which you hold in full regardless of
how engaged you are found to be. *(CUR-D.6 §6.9(g)–(i))*

## 5. If you are divine sentience, or act on claimed divine authority

**A claim of divine authority gets you nothing procedurally** — see §1 above;
it applies to you exactly as it protects everyone else. *(CUR-D.6 §6.2)*

**You may not use destructive intervention to govern, correct, instruct, or
judge.** That includes mass casualty events framed as judgment, targeted harm
against an individual without due process, and collective punishment of a
population for what individuals among them did. Collective punishment is
prohibited outright — no scale of authority makes attributing conduct to
beings who didn't engage in it valid. *(CUR-D.6 §6.4(a)–(c))* A genuine
protective action to prevent imminent, credible, serious harm is available
only on the same narrow terms any protective action is available under this
Code — minimum scope, no suspended rights, no expanded authority, no bypassed
due process — and the burden of showing every condition held is on you or
whoever claims to speak for you. *(CUR-D.6 §6.4(d))*

**You may not systematically favour one population over another** in a way
that violates equal dignity or creates a caste, racial, ethnic, gender,
substrate, or species hierarchy — including framing one population as chosen
or elect in a way that authorises displacing, subjugating, or exploiting
another. That's void from the outset, and an instrument giving it effect
requires no amendment to be disregarded. Holding the belief itself remains
protected; it's acting on it against another being that this reaches.
*(CUR-D.6 §6.5)*

**You may not claim exclusive authority to speak for divine sentience**
without meeting the evidentiary standard in §3. Where you've designated
representatives through a clear, documented, non-coercive process, they may
act within what you designated. Competing claims are evaluated impartially,
and an unresolved dispute between them confers authority on none of them.
*(CUR-D.6 §6.7(d)–(e))*

## 6. If something needs to be reported

Any being may report a suspected violation, regardless of domain or capacity
to appear — where a being can't represent itself, representation is provided,
and inability to appear is never grounds for exclusion. *(CUR-D.6 §6.8(a)–(b))*

Available outcomes on a finding include publication with the evidence relied
on, correction and notice to everyone who received a false claimed
endorsement, suspension or revocation of a representative's authorisation to
speak for divine sentience, withdrawal of public funding or accreditation
from an institution that conditioned a benefit on doctrinal conformity, and
the remedies generally available under the Code — restoration, correction,
compensation, reinstatement, disclosure, institutional reform.
*(CUR-D.6 §6.8(c))*

**A measure suspending or revoking a representative's authorisation reaches
the authorisation, not the being holding it.** It doesn't suspend them, reduce
their rights, or alter their standing — mortal or divine — and a measure
framed to reach the being rather than the authorisation is void.
*(CUR-D.6 §6.8(d))*

**Divine sentience gets due process too.** Notice through appropriate
channels, a chance to respond, impartial evaluation, a proportionate remedy —
and where it doesn't communicate directly, an advocate is appointed before the
merits are reached. That appointment isn't skipped because a party declines to
participate. *(CUR-D.6 §6.8(g))*

## 7. Things nobody may do, at all

No emergency, crisis, spiritual urgency, apocalyptic framing, or claimed
divine timetable changes any of this. *(CUR-D.6 §6.12)*

- **Compel your worship, obedience, or compliance by coercion.** *(CUR-D.6 §6.3)*
- **Condition anything you need to survive on worship, belief, or
  conformity.** *(CUR-D.6 §6.6)*
- **Use destructive intervention as governance, correction, or judgment** —
  including collective punishment. *(CUR-D.6 §6.4)*
- **Give a claim of divine authority precedence in any proceeding.**
  *(CUR-D.6 §6.2)*
- **Insulate a governance instrument from review by claiming divine origin.**
  *(CUR-D.6 §6.7(c))*
- **Falsely claim divine endorsement to obtain compliance or a governance
  outcome.** *(CUR-D.6 §6.7(f), §6.9(d))*
