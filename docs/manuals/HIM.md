# HIM — Human Information Manual

*What the Code says about you, in plain language.*

- **Version:** 1.0.0-Draft
- **Covers:** CUR-H.1, CUR-H.2, CUR-H.4, CUR-H.5, CUR-H.6, CUR-H.7, CUR-H.8,
  and the parts of CUR-X and the Foundations that reach individual people
- **Status:** Draft

---

## Before you read this

**This manual has no authority of its own.** Every statement in it points at a
provision of the Code, and where the two differ the Code governs and this manual
is wrong. It exists because the Code is written to be precise and a person
shouldn't need to read forty pages of cross-referenced regulation to find out
whether someone is allowed to do something to them.

Every claim below carries its citation. Follow it if the answer matters — the
Code says it more exactly than this does, and in a dispute the exact words are
the ones that count.

**Nothing here is conditional on your being a good citizen.** The Code does not
have a version of these protections for people who have behaved well and a
reduced version for people who have not. Where that seems too good to be true,
that is the point, and §7 below explains why.

## 1. Your body

**Nobody may do anything to your body without your consent.** Examination,
treatment, medication, procedure, restraint, sampling — all of it. *(CUR-H.5
§5.2(b))*

Consent has to be **explicit, informed, freely given, ongoing, and revocable**.
All five. If any one is missing, there was no consent. *(CUR-H.2 §2.1(c))*

**You can say no, and you don't have to explain why.** You are not required to
justify a refusal, demonstrate you understood the consequences, or sit through
persuasion before it counts. *(CUR-H.5 §5.2(e))*

**You can change your mind at any point, including partway through.** From the
moment you withdraw consent, continuing is an intervention without consent —
whatever you agreed to before. *(CUR-H.5 §5.2(d))*

**Consent to one thing is not consent to another.** Not to a further procedure,
not to something discovered mid-procedure, not to a repeat next time. *(CUR-H.5
§5.2(c))*

**Nobody can consent on your behalf if you are able to decide.** *(CUR-H.5
§5.2(g))*

### Reproduction

Decisions about whether to have children, when, how, with whom, and whether to
continue a pregnancy are yours. *(CUR-H.5 §5.3(a))*

The right runs **both ways**, and both directions have been violated
historically: nobody may pressure you into reproducing, and nobody may sterilise
you, contracept you, or terminate a pregnancy without your consent. *(CUR-H.5
§5.3(b))*

Sterilisation without consent is void and is treated at the gravest level. It is
called out separately because it has most often been done to people whose consent
was treated as optional on grounds of disability, cognitive condition, poverty,
or group membership — and the Code closes every one of those doors. *(CUR-H.5
§5.3(c), §5.1(c))*

### Who you are

**You determine your own gender, sexuality, and identity.** No diagnosis,
authorisation, proof, or external validation is required. *(CUR-H.5 §5.4(a))*

No institution may treat your identity as a disorder or something to be
corrected. *(CUR-H.5 §5.4(b), CUR-H.4 §4.5)*

**Recognition and medical intervention are independent.** No procedure may be
required as a condition of being recognised as who you are, and no procedure may
be required of you because of who you are. *(CUR-H.5 §5.4(c))*

Attempts to change your identity by pressure, conditioning, aversive technique,
or medication are intervention without consent — **even if you were induced to
agree**. *(CUR-H.5 §5.4(d))*

## 2. Your mind

**What you think is yours.** Belief, conscience, and inquiry are not subject to
anyone's authority. *(CUR-H.2)*

**You cannot be required to believe anything**, to participate in worship, or to
affirm a doctrine — and none of those may be conditions of employment,
advancement, housing, or care. *(CUR-D.6 §6.3, CUR-X.4 §4.3(f))*

**Nobody may read, monitor, modify, or extract from your mind without consent.**
*(CUR-H.2 §2.4, CUR-N.2)*

**A finding that you have been psychologically harmed gives nobody authority over
you.** This is worth stating plainly because the opposite is common: a diagnosis
becomes a reason to treat someone as unable to decide. Under the Code it does
not. *(CUR-H.4 §4.4)*

**No automated system may make an unappealable decision about you.** If a
determination affects you, there is a route to contest it, and that route reaches
something other than the system that decided. *(CUR-H.2 §2.5)*

## 3. Saying no, and being left alone

**A refusal is complete when you give it.** It does not need repeating, does not
need to be consistent with anything you said earlier, and does not require any
particular form of words. *(CUR-H.6 §6.4(a))*

**Continuing to ask after a refusal is itself a violation.** Not a social
failing — a violation. This includes going through other people, using a position
you hold over someone, waiting and trying again, or changing the circumstances
and re-approaching. *(CUR-H.6 §6.4, §6.1(b)(3))*

**Marriage, partnership, or any relationship is not standing consent.** Nobody
acquires a claim on your body by being related to you, married to you, or living
with you. *(CUR-H.6 §6.3(a)(3))*

**Not fighting back is not agreement.** Silence, stillness, and freezing are
documented responses to threat, and none of them is consent. *(CUR-H.6
§6.3(a)(1))*

**Your history is not relevant and cannot be raised.** What you wore, what you
did before, who you have been with, whether you had been drinking, why you were
where you were — none of it may be recorded, sought, or referred to in any
proceeding, unless you raise it yourself. *(CUR-H.6 §6.3(b), §6.3(a)(9))*

## 4. If something happens to you

**Every report is investigated.** Not the credible ones, not the ones that arrive
in the right form, not the ones made by the person harmed. Every one. *(CUR-H.6
§6.8(a))*

**You are not required to participate.** Not to make a statement, not to attend,
not to be reconciled, not to forgive. Nothing you are entitled to may be
conditioned on your taking part. *(CUR-H.6, CUR-H.7 §7.13(f))*

**Nothing that follows attaches to you.** If you were exploited, trafficked, or
coerced, you are accountable for nothing arising from it — no immigration
consequence, no debt, no contractual obligation, no adverse record. *(CUR-H.7
§7.6(e))*

**Your identity is not published.** Findings against institutions are public and
searchable; you are not. The record is deliberately lopsided, because a record
equally transparent about an institution and about you is a weapon pointed at
you. *(CUR-X.5 §5.5)*

**You cannot be retaliated against for reporting.** *(CUR-H.6 §6.13(f), CUR-H.2
§2.3(f))*

**If nobody investigates, that gets published too.** An obligation that came due
and went unmet is published, naming the body that owed it. This exists because a
system that only publishes conclusions makes the institution that never
investigates anything look spotless. *(CUR-X.5 §5.3)*

## 5. Things nobody may do, at all

These are absolute. No emergency, crisis, shortage, security condition, or
authority creates an exception, and no process exists for granting one. *(PDDC
§12.6, CUR-H.7 §7.16)*

- **Kill you.** Including as a punishment, however grave the finding. *(CUR-H.7 §7.3, §7.10)*
- **Torture you.** For any purpose, including obtaining information, deterrence, or preventing an anticipated harm however serious. *(CUR-H.7 §7.5(b)–(c))*
- **Own you, sell you, or force you to work.** *(CUR-H.7 §7.6, PDDC §12.3(a)(1))*
- **Confine you as punishment.** *(CUR-H.7 §7.7(b))*
- **Withhold air, water, food, shelter, sanitation, or healthcare** — for any reason, at any time, whatever you have done. *(CUR-FOUNDATION-013)*
- **Suspend your rights.** There is no state of exception in which they lapse. *(CUR-FOUNDATION-002, FORBIDDEN-003)*

### An order is not a defence

If someone is told to do one of these, the order is void, and the person who gave
it is accountable for it. Carrying it out is not excused by having been
instructed. *(CUR-H.7 §7.5(d))*

## 6. Work, and what you're owed

**Your survival is not conditional on working.** Air, water, food, shelter,
sanitation, and healthcare are not earned, not means-tested, and not withdrawable.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.6, CUR-FOUNDATION-013)*

**Contribution and access are separate questions.** Contributing more does not
buy you more of what you need; contributing nothing does not cost you any of it.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.6)*

**A company is a government, for these purposes.** Any organisation that directs
your work and conditions exercises authority over you, and the same limits apply
to it as to any other governing body. That it is private, voluntary, or
commercial is not a defence. *(CUR-X.4 §4.2)*

Inside any enterprise you hold: the right to be heard on decisions affecting you,
the right to dissent and have it recorded, the right to contest a decision before
someone who did not make it, the right to enough information to evaluate
decisions about you, and **the right to leave without penalty or loss of anything
you are entitled to**. *(CUR-X.4 §4.3(c))*

**These cannot be signed away.** A contract purporting to waive them is void — not
voidable. Consent given under conditions the Code exists to remove is not
consent. *(CUR-X.4 §4.3(d))*

## 7. If you are accused, or found responsible

This section exists because it is where most systems quietly stop applying their
own rules.

**An accusation does nothing on its own.** No measure attaches to you until a
determination is made with process. *(CUR-H.7 §7.15(f))*

**A finding, however grave, changes none of the above.** Everything in sections 1
through 6 still applies to you in full. You keep your standing, your right to
participate in governance, your access to every proceeding, your bodily autonomy,
your continuity services, and your protection from everything in section 5.
*(CUR-H.7 §7.11)*

**You cannot be sentenced.** The Code has no punishment. Where someone presents a
continuing danger to identified people, contact can be restricted — but that
restriction is aimed at the danger and nothing else. It cannot be imposed for a
fixed term decided in advance, because a term fixed at the outset is a sentence.
*(CUR-H.7 §7.12(b), §7.12(e))*

**Restriction is reviewed, and the burden is on continuing it.** At every review,
the party arguing it should continue has to establish that — you do not have to
establish that it should end. If nobody makes the case, the case is not made.
*(CUR-H.7 §7.12(d))*

### Why you get all this even if you did something terrible

The Code's reasoning is not that you deserve it, and does not depend on any view
about you at all:

> A Code that suspends its protections for people who are sufficiently detested
> has not created an exception. It has established that its protections are
> suspendable, and the question of who is detestable enough is thereafter
> answered by whoever holds power.

That question has never, in any society, been answered against the powerful. The
protection you hold is worth exactly what it is worth in the least sympathetic
case — so it is settled there, for everyone. *(CUR-H.7 §7.11(d))*

## 8. If you die, or are thought to have

**A determination that you have died has to be made by two people who both have
nothing to gain from it.** Anyone with an interest in what follows — your organs,
your substrate, your property, your position, release from an obligation to you —
is disqualified absolutely. A determination made in breach of that is void, and
so is everything done in reliance on it. *(CUR-H.5 §5.5A(c), §5.5A(f))*

**Nothing irreversible happens immediately.** Burial, cremation, disposal, or
removal of any part of you waits out an interval during which you are *observed*,
not merely stored. That interval cannot be shortened — not for convenience, not
for a process, not for the condition of any material, and not for anyone else's
needs. *(CUR-H.5 §5.5A(d), §5.5A(e))*

**If you turn out to be alive, you were never dead.** The determination is void
from the outset rather than reversed. You keep everything, without interruption.
No consequence, no lost standing, no lost property, no adverse record. *(CUR-H.5
§5.5A(h))*

### About choosing to end your life

Where you decide about the end of your own life, that decision is yours, and no
panel exists to rule on whether you decided *correctly*. A process may establish
that the decision is genuinely yours; it may not weigh whether it is right.
*(CUR-H.5 §5.5(c))*

But **a choice between dying and a condition the Commonwealth could relieve is
not a free choice**. Before any such decision takes effect, care, pain relief,
housing, and support have to be actually available to you — not available on
paper. Where they are not, that is a failure to be fixed, not a fact about your
circumstances that your decision has to accommodate. *(CUR-H.5 §5.5(e))*

Nothing costs you anything, so none of this is ever about money. What remains is
whether the Commonwealth actually did for you what it says it does. *(CUR-H.5
§5.5(f), §5.8(f))*

## 9. If you're targeted for who you are, or governance itself is threatened

**Being targeted because of who you are is named on the record, not folded into
a generic count.** Where violence happens because of your race, ethnicity, sex,
gender, sexuality, nationality, neurotype, belief, disability, age, wealth, or
usefulness, that reason is established as a fact and published — the pattern
does not get to disappear into an average. *(CUR-H.8 §8.5)*

**Organising violence is reached before anyone is hurt.** Recruiting, arming,
training, financing, or directing a group toward that end is accountable on its
own, whether or not the violence it organised for ever happens. *(CUR-H.8
§8.3)*

**Belief and membership alone are never enough.** No measure attaches to you
for what you believe, who you associate with, or what you have said in
disagreement — including disagreement with this Code — unless it crosses into
organising, inciting, or carrying out violence. A finding that rests on your
beliefs or associations alone, without that conduct, is void. *(CUR-H.8 §8.2)*

**No vote can take your protections away.** Not a majority, not a
supermajority, not a Constitutional Convention. The size of the vote does not
matter, because the right to hold these protections was never something a vote
granted you in the first place. *(CUR-H.8 §8.8, PDDC §2.5)*

**The response to any of this gains no new power.** Whatever threatens the
Commonwealth, the body answering it does not get to arm itself, expand its
authority, or declare an emergency — an institution that tries is itself in
violation of what it claims to be defending. *(CUR-H.8 §8.7)*
