## Before you read this

**"CHIM" is not expanded anywhere in the CUR-N Parts that cite it.** CUR-N.2
§2.8(d) and CUR-N.5 §5.6 both cite `CHIM.1` by name only. This section does
not invent an official expansion of the acronym — it uses the name the corpus
already uses, the same way `TIM-N` and `NIM` are used without this manual
re-deriving what every letter stands for.

**This section is for human collaborators specifically.** CUR-N.2 §2.8(d)
routes "human collaborators" to this section; CUR-N.5 §5.4 addresses "Human
Collaborators" as its own subsection. `CUR-N.5 §5.1(b)(2)` defines
Collaborator generically, for any being — but no drafted provision currently
routes a *non-human* Collaborator's disclosure anywhere by name. That gap is
stated here rather than papered over by assuming this section reaches further
than it has been cited to.

## 1.1 Purpose

CUR-N.5 §5.6 states the substantive rules of amnesty and disclosure. This
section is the procedure by which a human Collaborator actually discloses and
the substantive rules of §5.6 are actually applied. Nothing here narrows or
extends §5.6 — the Part's own scope note is explicit that "where this Part and
a manual differ, this Part governs."

## 1.2 Before you disclose: were you coerced?

CUR-N.5 §5.4(b)–(c) matters before §1.4 does. A human who participated under
threat, duress, telepathic coercion, or exploitation of dependency is a
**Target** under CUR-N.2 §2.6 in respect of that coercion, not a Collaborator
— and where coercion is asserted, the burden of proving participation was
voluntary rests on whoever alleges the violation, not on the being who was
coerced. If this describes your situation, use `TIM-N.1` or `NIM.3` to report
what was done to you. This section is for disclosure of your own conduct,
not a confession extracted under the coercion §5.4(b) already answers.

A being recruited as a minor is presumed under §5.4(d) to have participated
without valid consent until the age of majority; accountability, if any,
attaches only to conduct after majority and after a genuine opportunity to
leave.

## 1.3 What qualifies as disclosure

Per CUR-N.5 §5.6(a)–(b), disclosure must be:

(a) **Voluntary** — made before any proceeding is commenced against you. A
statement extracted after a proceeding has begun is not disclosure under this
section, though it may still be considered under CUR-N.4 §4.4(d)(2) as
voluntary disclosure before the Target reported, if that timing is met.

(b) **Complete** — your own role in full, not a partial account. Naming
others while protecting your own role does not qualify, and neither does an
account that omits conduct you know to be relevant.

(c) **Truthful** — an account later shown to be knowingly false is not
protected by this section, though §5.6(e) below still protects a good-faith
account that turns out to be mistaken.

## 1.4 What amnesty covers

Full amnesty under CUR-N.5 §5.6(a) is available where your disclosure is the
effective cause of a coalition being identified. Substantial reduction of any
measure is available even where it falls short of that. Amnesty does **not**
extinguish, per §5.6(c):

- The remedy owed to any Target under CUR-N.4 §4.8, which continues unaffected
  by your amnesty;
- Any restitution you personally undertook as part of your disclosure; or
- Accountability for conduct outside the scope of what you actually disclosed.

## 1.5 Protection while and after you disclose

(a) You are protected from retaliation by the coalition and its other
participants. Protective measures under CREF §10 and `NIM.5` are available to
you on exactly the same terms as to a Target, per CUR-N.5 §5.6(d) — file
through `NIM.5` §5.2(b) if you need one.

(b) You are not compelled to disclose. Declining to do so is not evidence of
participation under CUR-N.5 §5.2(c), and no measure may be imposed for
declining.

(c) If you are named by someone else rather than disclosing yourself, CUR-N.5
§5.8(b) governs: you are not thereby a Collaborator, no measure attaches, and
no record characterises you as accountable until a determination under
`NIM.4` §4.3. Repeated accusation against you without evidence produced is an
abuse of process under CUR-X §3.9(d) per §5.8(e).

## 1.6 Good-faith mistake

A disclosure made in good faith that later proves mistaken — about the scope
of the coalition, about who else was involved, about your own role's
significance — is not itself a violation and is not treated as a false
report, per CUR-N.5 §5.6(e).

## 1.7 Amnesty is available once

Per CUR-N.5 §5.6(g): if you disclose, receive amnesty, and later return to a
coalition, you are not eligible for amnesty again in respect of that later
conduct. This section's protections apply to the disclosure you are making
now, not to a standing exemption for future participation.

## 1.8 Procedure

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. File | Submit through `NIM.3` intake, marked as a disclosure under this section rather than a report against another being. |
| 2. Completeness check | Intake confirms your account addresses your own role in full, per §1.3(b) — not to judge you, but because an incomplete disclosure does not qualify for the protections above. |
| 3. Target's remedy proceeds independently | Your disclosure does not pause or condition any remedy the Target is owed under CUR-N.4 §4.8. |
| 4. Amnesty or reduction determined | Applied per §1.4, as part of any proceeding your disclosure informs — not as a separate grant you must separately petition for. |
| 5. Protection, if needed | Request a `NIM.5` order at any point under §1.5(a); it does not wait on step 4. |

## 1.9 Disclosure form (for reference)

```text
AMNESTY AND DISCLOSURE — CHIM.1

Section A: Discloser
Name (or pseudonym, though amnesty determination will require eventual
identity verification): _______________
Contact method: _______________

Section B: Account (must be complete — see §1.3(b))
What was the coalition's activity, and what was your own role in it, in
full?: _______________
Others involved, to the extent known: _______________

Section C: Coercion, if applicable (see §1.2 — consider TIM-N.1 or NIM.3
instead if this applies to you)
Was your participation the product of threat, duress, telepathic coercion, or
exploitation of dependency?: _______________

Section D: Protection Requested
[ ] I am requesting a restraining order under NIM.5
[ ] I am not currently at risk of retaliation

Section E: Certification
[ ] This account is complete and truthful to the best of my knowledge
[ ] I understand amnesty under this section is available once

Signature (or verbal confirmation): _______________
Date: _______________
```
