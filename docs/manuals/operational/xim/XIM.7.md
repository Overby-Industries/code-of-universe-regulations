## Before you read this

**This is not [`docs/manuals/XIM.md`](../../XIM.md).** That document is the
Cross-Domain *Information* Manual, covering CUR-X.1, CUR-X.3, and CUR-X.5 in
plain language. This is a numbered operational section, cited by name — the
same relationship `NIM.3`–`NIM.5` bear to `docs/manuals/NIM.md`. It has no
sections numbered 1 through 6; none is cited by any drafted Part, and none is
invented here to fill a gap nothing requires filled.

**Only one provision actually names this Board.** CUR-N.2 §2.5(e) names it
directly: "the Telepathic Incident Review Board established under XIM.7."
CUR-N.4 §4.11(d) and CUR-N.5 §5.9(e) each require a protective action to be
"reviewed within seventy-two (72) hours" — the identical clock — but neither
names a reviewing body. This section designates the same Board for those two
triggers rather than leaving "reviewed by whom" unanswered, on the reasoning
that a second, differently-composed body reviewing the same class of action
on the same clock would invite inconsistent outcomes for no stated purpose.
That designation is this manual's own operational choice, not a restatement
of something CUR-N.4 or CUR-N.5 already says — §7.2 marks it accordingly.

**Why this Board is numbered under XIM rather than NIM.** Every actual and
designated trigger for this Board comes from the Non-Human Cognitive Actors
domain, and an argument could be made for numbering it `NIM.6` instead. It is
placed under `XIM` because a body that reviews a CREF §10 protective action is
not, in its function, specific to telepathic invasion — it is the general
shape of review CREF §15 requires for any protective action taken before a
determination, applied here to the one domain that currently generates them.
If another domain's Part comes to cite a protective-action review with the
same 72-hour structure, this section is written so it can be extended rather
than duplicated. Until that happens, its actual jurisdiction is the four
triggers at §7.2, one cited directly and three by this manual's own
designation, and this note exists so that distinction is not lost.

## 7.1 Purpose

Four provisions require review of a protective action within seventy-two (72)
hours: CUR-N.2 §2.5(e) (use of the protective-action exception), CUR-N.4
§4.11(d) (a protective action taken before a determination generally), CUR-N.5
§5.9(e) (a protective action taken in response to coalition activity), and
`NIM.5` §5.3(d) (a provisional restraining order). This section is the single
procedure that reviews all four.

## 7.2 What triggers a review

| Trigger | Clock starts | This Board named by |
|---|---|---|
| A telepathic intrusion claimed under the §2.5 protective-action exception | The intrusion itself | CUR-N.2 §2.5(e), directly |
| Any protective action under CREF §10 taken before a CUR-N.4 determination | The action itself | CUR-N.4 §4.11(d) requires the review; this section designates the Board to perform it |
| A protective action taken in response to coalition activity in progress | The action itself | CUR-N.5 §5.9(e) requires the review, itself pointing to §4.11(d); this section designates the Board to perform it |
| A provisional restraining order | Issuance | `NIM.5` §5.3(d), this manual's own cross-reference |

In every case the seventy-two hours runs from the act, not from the report of
it — reported late under `NIM.3` §3.4, the review window is not extended, and
a late report narrows the Board's own time to act before the clock runs out.

## 7.3 Composition

(a) The Board has no involvement in, and no interest in the outcome of, the
action under review — the same standard CUR-N.4 §4.3(b)(3) requires of the
body conducting a full determination, and the same standard `NIM.4` §4.4
applies to it.

(b) A member who took, authorised, or would be a party to any later
proceeding concerning the specific action under review does not sit on that
review.

(c) Where the acting being or the affected being cannot participate directly,
representation is available on the same terms as CUR-N.4 §4.3(b)(4) — an
independent advocate under CUR-X §3.7.

## 7.4 Standard of review

(a) For a review triggered under CUR-N.2 §2.5(e), the Board determines whether
every condition in §2.5(b) held at the time of the intrusion: an imminent,
credible threat of death or serious physical harm; the intrusion strictly
limited to what was necessary to prevent that specific harm; no less intrusive
means available; and the report made under `NIM.3` §3.4. The burden rests on
the being who acted, per §2.5(f) — the Board does not presume justification
and require it be disproven.

(b) For every trigger, the Board additionally confirms the general limits
CREF §10 places on any protective action: limited to the minimum scope
necessary, no suspension of any right, no expansion of any authority, and no
bypass of due process protections that will apply once the matter reaches a
full determination.

(c) The Board's review is not a determination of whether a violation
occurred. It is a determination of whether the protective action itself was,
and remains, justified. Those are different findings and neither substitutes
for the other.

## 7.5 Outcomes

The four triggers in §7.2 fall into two kinds — a protective action or
provisional order is ongoing and can be confirmed, narrowed, or left to lapse;
a §2.5 intrusion is already complete by the time it is reviewed and can only
be found justified or not. The outcomes below are grouped accordingly.

**For an ongoing protective action or provisional order** (CUR-N.4 §4.11(d),
CUR-N.5 §5.9(e), `NIM.5` §5.3):

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | The action or order stands as taken, pending the outcome of any determination under `NIM.4`. |
| **Confirmed, narrowed** | The action continues but its scope is reduced to what the Board finds was actually justified. |
| **Not confirmed within 72 hours** | The action or order lapses automatically, per CUR-N.4 §4.11(d)'s own text — "A provisional measure that is not confirmed within that period lapses" — applied by `NIM.5` §5.3(d) to a provisional order on the same terms. No further step is required to end it. |

**For a completed intrusion claimed under the §2.5 protective-action
exception:**

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Justified** | The four conditions of §2.5(b) are found to have held. No further step follows from this section. |
| **Found unjustified** | CUR-N.2 §2.5(e)'s own text governs: "Unjustified use is a violation of §2.2 and is not mitigated by having been claimed under this section." The finding opens a proceeding under `NIM.4` against the being who acted, on the same terms as any other invasion. |
| **Review not completed within 72 hours** | CUR-N.2 §2.5(e) does not state a consequence for this case, unlike §4.11(d) — an intrusion that has already occurred has nothing left to lapse. This section does not invent one. The review remains owed and proceeds late; lateness neither excuses the intrusion nor substitutes for the finding §2.5(e) requires. |

## 7.6 This Board grants no authority

Consistent with `TIM-N.5`'s Constitutional Scope Note and with CREF §10
itself, nothing in this section creates emergency powers, expands any
institution's mandate, or permits the Board to act against any being beyond
reviewing the specific action before it. The Board reviews; it does not
authorise in advance, and a request to pre-clear an intrusion is not a
function this section performs.

## 7.7 Records

Every review — trigger, composition, standard applied, and outcome — is
logged as a constitutional event under CUR-FOUNDATION-005 and
CUR-FOUNDATION-010 §14, on the same terms as a determination under `NIM.4`
§4.11. Records identifying a Target are held under CUR-N.2 §2.6(e).

## 7.8 Relationship to other sections

| Section | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `NIM.3` §3.4 | Where a protective-action report is filed, starting the process this section reviews |
| `NIM.4` | Where a review outcome of "found unjustified" proceeds |
| `NIM.5` §5.3 | Provisional restraining orders, one of this Board's four triggers |
| `TIM-N.5` | The crisis measures a being takes before any report or review reaches this Board |
| `CHIM.1` | Not reviewed here — amnesty and disclosure are governed by their own procedure, not by this Board |
