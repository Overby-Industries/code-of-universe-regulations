## 5.1 Purpose and two forms of order

CUR-N.2 §2.6(c)(1) states the right to a telepathic restraining order as a
protection available to a Target. CUR-N.4 §4.5 lists a restraining order as
ordinarily available from Third Degree upward as a measure following a
determination. CUR-N.5 §5.6(d) extends the same protection to a discloser
under `CHIM.1`. Those are, procedurally, two different things, and this
section keeps them separate:

| Form | Available when | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| **Provisional order** | Immediately, before any determination, where contact is ongoing or imminent | §5.3 — a protective action under CREF §10, reviewed by `XIM.7` within 72 hours |
| **Order as a measure** | Following a determination under `NIM.4`, Third Degree or above | §5.4 — issued as part of the determination itself, under CUR-N.4 §4.5–§4.6 |

A being may hold a provisional order while a determination is pending, and the
provisional order is superseded by whatever the determination under §5.4
produces — confirmed as a full measure, narrowed, or lapsed if the finding
does not support one.

## 5.2 Who may petition

(a) A Target, as defined at CUR-N.2 §2.6.

(b) A discloser under `CHIM.1`, on the same terms as a Target, per CUR-N.5
§5.6(d).

(c) A guardian or advocate on behalf of a being who cannot petition directly,
consistent with `TIM-N.1` §2's guardian provisions.

(d) Nothing in this section requires the petitioner to have previously
reported, resisted, or shielded. CUR-N.2 §2.11(e) is addressed directly at
§5.7 below because it is the provision most often misapplied against a
petitioner under this section specifically.

## 5.3 Provisional order procedure

(a) File through `NIM.3` intake, marking the petition urgent. Filing does not
require a completed incident report — `TIM-N.5` §5.4.2 (Documentation) states
that documentation may follow recovery, and the same applies here.

(b) State: the being from whom protection is sought, if known; the conduct
giving rise to the request; and whether contact is ongoing, expected, or
already ceased.

(c) A provisional order under this subsection is a protective action under
CREF §10. It is not an emergency measure, confers no authority beyond
restricting the named being's telepathic contact with the petitioner, and does
not suspend any right of the being it is issued against — including that
being's own entitlement to notice and response once the matter reaches a
determination under `NIM.4`.

(d) The order takes effect on issuance and is reviewed by the Telepathic
Incident Review Board under `XIM.7` within seventy-two (72) hours, per the
same clock CUR-N.2 §2.5(e) and CUR-N.4 §4.11(d) apply to every other
protective action in this domain. An order not confirmed within that window
lapses.

(e) Confirmation under `XIM.7` does not itself determine that a violation
occurred. It determines only that the provisional order was, and remains,
justified pending a full determination under `NIM.4`.

## 5.4 Order as a measure

(a) Where a determination under `NIM.4` finds Third Degree or above, the body
conducting the determination may include a restraining order in the measure
imposed, per CUR-N.4 §4.5(a).

(b) The order is subject to CUR-N.4 §4.6 in full: it may not suspend a right
of the being it is issued against, may not attach to that being's family or
associates, may not withhold a Vital Continuity Service from anyone, and may
not run without scheduled review.

(c) Where a provisional order under §5.3 already exists, the determination
either confirms it as the measure, narrows its scope, or allows it to lapse if
the finding does not support one. It is not reissued from nothing.

## 5.5 What an order restricts, and what it does not

Consistent with the isolation limits at CUR-N.4 §4.7(b)–(c), a restraining
order under this section restricts contact between the named being and the
petitioner. It restricts nothing else: the named being retains every Vital
Continuity Service, every right of due process in any proceeding concerning
the order itself, and every PDDC §10.1 right, without interruption.

## 5.6 Duration and review

(a) A provisional order under §5.3 is time-limited to the 72-hour review
window unless confirmed.

(b) A confirmed order or an order issued as a measure states a duration and a
review interval no greater than the interval that would apply to any
comparable measure under CUR-N.4 §4.5–§4.7. It does not run indefinitely.

(c) The burden at any review of continuing an order rests on the party seeking
to continue it, mirroring CUR-N.4 §4.7(d) for isolation.

## 5.7 The complacency defence does not apply here

CUR-N.2 §2.11(e) states directly: "A restraining order petition shall not be
denied on grounds of complainant complacency." Accordingly, a petition under
this section is not weakened, delayed, or denied because the petitioner did
not report promptly, did not resist, was not continuously vigilant, or had
previously consented to contact. Past consent does not imply present consent,
per CUR-N.2 §2.1(c)(3)–(4), and prior contact is not a ground to deny
protection from future contact.

## 5.8 Petition form (for reference)

```text
TELEPATHIC RESTRAINING ORDER PETITION — NIM.5

Section A: Petitioner (optional for anonymous provisional filing; required to
receive the order once issued)
Name (or pseudonym): _______________
Contact method: _______________
Relationship to Target, if petitioning on another's behalf: _______________

Section B: Named Being (if known)
Description (species, claimed identity, prior contact): _______________

Section C: Basis for the Order
What happened, and is contact ongoing, expected, or ceased?: _______________
Is this a provisional filing (§5.3) or made as part of a pending
determination under NIM.4 (§5.4)?: _______________

Section D: Scope Requested
[ ] No telepathic contact of any kind
[ ] No contact within a stated location or circumstance: _______________
[ ] Other: _______________

Section E: Consent for Follow-Up
[ ] I consent to being contacted using the method above
[ ] I request the review under XIM.7 proceed without further contact with me

Signature (or verbal confirmation): _______________
Date: _______________
```

## 5.9 Relationship to other sections

| Section | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `NIM.3` | Where a petition is filed |
| `NIM.4` | The determination that confirms, narrows, or supersedes a provisional order |
| `XIM.7` | Reviews every provisional order within 72 hours |
| `CHIM.1` | Extends this section's protection to a discloser, on the same terms as a Target |
| `TIM-N.5` | Immediate crisis measures while a provisional order is being filed |
