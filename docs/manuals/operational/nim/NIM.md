## Before you read this

**This is not [`docs/manuals/NIM.md`](../../NIM.md).** That document is the
Non-Human Cognitive Actor *Information* Manual — a plain-language explainer of
what CUR-N.2, CUR-N.4, and CUR-N.5 mean for the person reading them, at the
same register as HIM and SIM. This document is the *Operational* Manual: the
step-by-step procedure behind three specific mechanisms CUR-N.2 and CUR-N.4
name and leave to be written elsewhere — the Ombuds intake channel, the
accountability procedure, and the restraining-order petition. Where the two
disagree, the CUR-N Parts govern and both manuals are wrong; see
[`docs/manuals/NIM.md` §7](../../NIM.md#7-where-to-look-next) for the general
reading rule.

**This manual does not restate CUR-N.4's substance.** CUR-N.4's own scope note
says so directly: "the operational procedures sit in NIM.4 and NIM.5... Where
this Part and a manual differ, this Part governs." Every rule of substance —
what a Responsible Being is entitled to, what a measure may never do, how a
degree is determined — lives in CUR-N.4 and is cited here, not repeated. What
this manual adds is mechanics: forms, timelines, who does what in what order.

## NIM.0 — Introduction and Scope

### 0.1 Purpose

CUR-N.2 §2.8(a) names three channels through which a suspected violation may
be reported: the Ombudsman Office for Cognitive Sovereignty, "any designated
NIM reporting channel," and the Compliance Monitoring Network. `NIM.3` is that
designated channel. CUR-N.4 states what a Responsible Being is owed and what a
measure may never do; `NIM.4` is the procedure that gets a report from intake
to a determination under those rules. CUR-N.2 §2.6(c)(1) and CUR-N.4 §4.5
(Third Degree and above) make a restraining order an available protection and
measure; `NIM.5` is the petition itself.

### 0.2 Who this manual is for

| User | Relevant section |
|---|---|
| A being reporting a suspected violation of CUR-N.2 or CUR-N.5, other than through `TIM-N.1` | `NIM.3` |
| A being who took protective action under CUR-N.2 §2.5 and must report it within the hour | `NIM.3` §3.4 |
| A Target seeking accountability against a Responsible Being | `NIM.3` (to open), then `NIM.4` |
| A being named as a Responsible Being or a Collaborator | `NIM.4` §4.3 (your entitlements) |
| A Target or discloser seeking protection from further contact | `NIM.5` |
| Ombuds Office intake staff, investigators, or the body conducting a determination | All three, in sequence |

### 0.3 How this manual relates to TIM-N

`TIM-N` is the reporting and crisis-response manual for telepathic invasion
specifically, drafted first because CUR-N.2 cites it directly and because
crisis response could not wait on the rest of the domain's procedure being
written. `NIM.3` does not replace `TIM-N.1` — a being reporting a telepathic
invasion uses `TIM-N.1`, and `TIM-N.1` routes into the same Ombuds intake this
manual describes. `NIM.3` exists for reports `TIM-N.1` does not cover: a
suspected secret coalition under CUR-N.5, a protective-action report under
CUR-N.2 §2.5(b)(4), and any report made through the Compliance Monitoring
Network rather than the incident-specific form. `NIM.4` and `NIM.5` pick up
after intake regardless of which door the report came through.

### 0.4 Core principles

These are stated in `TIM-N.0` §0.3 for incident reporting and repeated here
because they apply without change to every procedure in this manual:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A report is not a finding | Nothing described in `NIM.3` determines anything. Only the procedure in `NIM.4` does, and only with full due process. |
| Strict liability is not summary process | CUR-N.2 §2.11(b)(3) removes the need to prove intent. CUR-N.4 §4.3(c) is explicit that it removes nothing else. `NIM.4` is written to that line. |
| No collective consequence | CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(6) and CUR-N.5 §5.5(e) forbid a measure or an inference reaching anyone but the being determined accountable. Every form in this manual asks about one being's conduct, never about their community, species, or associates. |
| No emergency shortcut | CUR-N.2, CUR-N.4 §4.11, and CUR-N.5 §5.9 each state independently that urgency changes the order of steps and never who holds authority. `NIM.3` §3.4 and `NIM.5` §5.6 are built on CREF §10, not on any emergency power, because none exists to invoke. |
