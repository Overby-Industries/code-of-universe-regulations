## 4.1 Purpose and relationship to CUR-N.4

CUR-N.4 states what a determination must contain, what a measure may never
do, and what a Responsible Being is entitled to. This section states the
sequence: how a report classified under `NIM.3` §3.5 becomes a determination.
CUR-N.4's own scope note governs this relationship: "the operational
procedures sit in NIM.4 and NIM.5... Where this Part and a manual differ, this
Part governs." Nothing below narrows, extends, or restates a right or a limit
stated in CUR-N.4 — where a step here appears to do so, CUR-N.4 controls and
this section is wrong.

## 4.2 Who this section is for

| User | What you need |
|---|---|
| A Target seeking accountability | §4.3 — how a proceeding is opened |
| A being named as a Responsible Being or Facilitator | §4.4 — your entitlements before anything is decided |
| The body conducting a determination | §4.5–§4.7 — the sequence, in order |
| A being subject to a measure, or seeking review of one | §4.9 — appeals |

## 4.3 Opening a proceeding

(a) A proceeding under CUR-N.4 opens on: a report classified under `NIM.3`
§3.5(2) as naming an identified being, a referral from `TIM-N.1` under its own
§4 step 4, or a finding from CUR-N.5 §5.2B routine monitoring sufficient to
identify a specific being's conduct.

(b) Opening a proceeding is not itself a finding. CUR-N.4 §4.2(a) makes
invasion a strict-liability violation once proven; it does not relieve the
proceeding of the burden of proving the conduct occurred, per §4.3(c) of that
Part.

(c) The being named is notified per §4.4(a) below before any measure of any
kind may attach, per CUR-N.4 §4.3(d) (a determination made without notice is
void) and CUR-N.5 §5.8(b) (a being named in a report is not thereby
accountable).

## 4.4 What the Responsible Being is entitled to

This restates CUR-N.4 §4.3(b) as a checklist for the body conducting the
proceeding. Every item is mandatory and none is waivable per §4.3(c):

- [ ] **Notice** — the conduct alleged, the degree asserted under CUR-N.2
  §2.3(a), and the measures sought, delivered before any measure attaches
- [ ] **An opportunity to respond** — through a channel adapted to the being's
  actual means of communication, which for a non-human cognitive actor may not
  be verbal or written in any human-legible form
- [ ] **Impartial evaluation** — by a body with no interest in the outcome;
  the same standard `XIM.7` §7.3 applies to protective-action review
- [ ] **Representation** — an independent advocate under CUR-X §3.7 is
  appointed where the being cannot represent itself or does not communicate
  directly, and appointment is not waived by the being's non-participation
  (CUR-N.4 §4.3(e))
- [ ] **A written determination** — stating findings, degree, measure,
  duration, and the route to appeal under §4.8 below

Where the being named declines to participate, the proceeding may continue
under §4.3(e) of CUR-N.4, but the advocate requirement above still applies in
full.

## 4.5 Determining the degree

(a) The degree is fixed by conduct against the table at CUR-N.2 §2.3(a) —
Fifth through First — and where conduct spans more than one degree, the
highest degree engaged governs per CUR-N.4 §4.4(b), with the full course of
conduct described on the record.

(b) Aggravating factors are those at CUR-N.2 §2.4(a). Advancing the
complacency defence against the Target is itself an aggravating factor per
§2.11(c) and shall be recorded as such, not merely rejected.

(c) Mitigating factors are limited to those at CUR-N.4 §4.4(d): cessation on
request before any proceeding, voluntary disclosure before the Target
reported, genuine incapacity (which reduces measure, never negates violation),
completed training under `TIM-N.2` for unintentional emission, and restitution
accepted by the Target. Nothing else mitigates; CUR-N.4 §4.4(e) is explicit
that the Target's conduct or characteristics never do.

## 4.6 Selecting a measure

(a) Use the table at CUR-N.4 §4.5(a) to identify what is ordinarily available
for the degree found, and impose the least restrictive measure sufficient to
prevent recurrence — never the table entry by default.

(b) Before finalising a measure, confirm it does not do any of the seven
things CUR-N.4 §4.6(a) forbids absolutely: suspend a right, alter
constitutional standing, withhold a Vital Continuity Service, modify the
Responsible Being's own cognition, run without scheduled review, attach to
anyone but the Responsible Being, or substitute for withdrawing an
authorisation where that is the actual proper subject.

(c) Where the measure includes a restraining order (ordinarily available from
Third Degree per CUR-N.4 §4.5(a)), open `NIM.5` as part of the same
determination rather than as a separate proceeding.

(d) Where the measure is isolation (First Degree, or repeated Second Degree
after lesser measures failed), follow `NIM.4` §4.7 below in addition to this
subsection.

## 4.7 Isolation, specifically

Isolation carries its own procedure because CUR-N.4 §4.7 states limits that
apply nowhere else in this Part:

(a) State a maximum duration in the determination itself. Isolation without a
stated maximum is not available under §4.7(e).

(b) Schedule review at intervals no greater than thirty (30) days, by a body
with no involvement in the original determination, per §4.7(d). The burden at
each review rests on whoever seeks to continue the isolation, not on the
isolated being to justify release.

(c) Confirm and record, at the point of imposition, that the isolated being
retains every item in CUR-N.4 §4.7(c): every Vital Continuity Service without
interruption, communication with counsel and any review body, the right to
bring and defend proceedings including this one's own appeal, access to
information and materials subject only to restrictions actually necessary to
prevent further invasion, and every PDDC §10.1 right in full.

(d) Isolation is not, and may not be administered as, punishment. Where
conditions of isolation risk psychological harm within CUR-H.4 §4.1(b)(1),
CUR-N.4 §4.7(g) applies and the conditions are auditable under
CUR-FOUNDATION-010.

## 4.8 Remedy to the Target

CUR-N.4 §4.8 governs and is not conditioned on any measure above:

(a) Begin remedy under CREF §12 on a finding of violation, whether or not the
Responsible Being has been identified, located, or is subject to any
proceeding at all.

(b) Available remedies — restoration, cognitive shielding assistance, record
correction, compensation, public disclosure at the Target's election, and
institutional reform where an institution was involved — do not require the
Target's participation in the proceeding against the Responsible Being, and
the Target's declining to participate carries no adverse consequence.

(c) Restoration precedes assignment of fault wherever reasonably possible,
consistent with CREF §12.

## 4.9 Appeals

(a) Every determination and measure is appealable under CREF §15 (Citizen
Review, Constitutional Court, or Independent Panels).

(b) An appeal does not suspend the Target's remedy under §4.8.

(c) A measure other than isolation continues pending appeal unless the
reviewing body orders otherwise. Isolation pending appeal requires the
reviewing body's affirmative confirmation within seven (7) days; failing that
confirmation, it lapses automatically per CUR-N.4 §4.10(c).

(d) A determination may be reopened on new evidence, misapplied degree, or a
measure that has become disproportionate, per §4.10(d).

## 4.10 Facilitators and institutions

Where the proceeding concerns a Facilitator (CUR-N.4 §4.1(b)(4)) or an
institution rather than the being who performed the invasion directly, run
§4.3–§4.9 above in full against the Facilitator or institution's own conduct.
CUR-N.4 §4.9(c) is explicit that diffusion of responsibility across a
structure is not a defence, and §4.9(e) routes any coalition-adjacent
facilitation to CUR-N.5, whose own §5.6 in turn makes `CHIM.1` available
where the Facilitator is human.

## 4.11 Records

Every step above — report, notice, response, determination, measure, review,
and appeal — is logged per CUR-N.4 §4.12(a) and CUR-FOUNDATION-010 §14: actor,
affected beings, degree, ground relied upon, FSM state at the time, and
outcome. An unrecorded measure did not lawfully occur and is itself a Class II
fault under §4.12(b). Records identifying a Target are held under CUR-N.2
§2.6(e); aggregate data is published to the Public Transparency Registry under
CUR-FOUNDATION-010 §9 without identifying any Target.

## 4.12 No emergency exception

Nothing above is altered by urgency. CUR-N.4 §4.11 states this in full: no
characterisation of an invasion as ongoing, severe, or unprecedented narrows
§4.3 or §4.6. Where an invasion is genuinely in progress, the available
instrument is a protective action under CREF §10 together with `TIM-N.5`'s
immediate measures — reported under `NIM.3` §3.4 and reviewed under `XIM.7`
within seventy-two (72) hours — not a shortcut through this section.
