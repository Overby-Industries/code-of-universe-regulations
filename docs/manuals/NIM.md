# NIM — Non-Human Cognitive Actor Information Manual

*What the Code says about your mind, in plain language.*

- **Version:** 1.0.0-Draft
- **Covers:** CUR-N.2, CUR-N.4, CUR-N.5
- **Status:** Draft

---

## Before you read this

**This manual has no authority of its own.** Every statement in it points at a
provision of the Code, and where the two differ the Code governs and this manual
is wrong. CUR-N.2 protects every conscious being's interior against invasion by
any other — human, silicon-based, animal, non-human cognitive actor, or deity.
This manual states that protection, what happens if you're accused of breaching
it, and what happens if you're named as part of a coalition that concealed one.

Every claim below carries its citation. Follow it if the answer matters — the
Code says it more exactly than this does.

---

## 1. Your mind is yours

**Nobody may access, project into, extract from, or manipulate your interior
cognitive or experiential state without your consent.** Reading thoughts or
memories, projecting images or voices, extracting emotional content, inducing
dreams, altering or implanting memories, establishing a link — all of it.
*(CUR-N.2 §2.1(a)–(b))*

Consent has to be **explicit, informed, ongoing, and revocable**. Given under
threat, duress, or a power imbalance — including a claim of divine authority —
it is void. *(CUR-N.2 §2.1(c))*

**This holds regardless of who is asking or why.** Claimed authority, claimed
superiority, a relationship you had before, a benevolent purpose, a claimed
enforcement or protective motive — none of it matters. It holds in every state
you might be in, including unconscious, dreaming, or altered. *(CUR-N.2 §2.2)*

**"They should have known better" is not a defence, and it doesn't run the
other way either.** A being capable of telepathic access is presumed capable
of understanding the prohibition. *(CUR-N.2 §2.2(c))* And nothing about *you* —
not your vigilance, your awareness, your shielding, your prior reports, or
your consciousness at the time — is ever relevant to whether what happened to
you was a violation. See §3 below.

## 2. What almost never justifies entry

A narrow protective-action exception exists. It is not an emergency power —
the Commonwealth recognises none — and it is not a licence.

All four of the following have to hold, every time:

1. An imminent, credible threat of death or serious physical harm, to you or
   to others;
2. The intrusion strictly limited to what's needed to prevent that specific
   harm;
3. No less intrusive means available — a verbal warning, physical
   intervention, a technological alert; and
4. Immediate report to the accountability body afterward.

*(CUR-N.2 §2.5(b))*

**The burden of proving all four is on the being who acted, not on you.** An
intrusion that can't be shown to satisfy every one of them was an invasion,
full stop. *(CUR-N.2 §2.5(f))* It has to be reviewed within 72 hours, and
using it and being wrong about it is itself a violation — claiming the
exception doesn't soften that. *(CUR-N.2 §2.5(e))*

What it never covers: ongoing monitoring, extraction of anything unrelated to
the specific threat, projecting anything threatening or tormenting, or a
justification supplied after the fact. *(CUR-N.2 §2.5(d))*

## 3. If you've been invaded

**Nothing about how you responded, or didn't, matters.** Not resisting, not
shielding, not knowing, not reporting quickly, having consented before, being
distracted, being asleep — none of it is a defence available to the being who
invaded you, and none of it reduces what you're owed. The responsibility is
entirely theirs. *(CUR-N.2 §2.11)*

**It doesn't matter whether they meant to.** Telepathic invasion is a strict
liability violation — nobody has to prove intent for it to have occurred.
Intent, when it's there, makes things worse for them, never better.
*(CUR-N.2 §2.11(b)(3), CUR-N.4 §4.2(a)–(b))*

You hold, without qualification:

- **The right to document it** — to record and share what happened, without
  retaliation or disbelief. *(CUR-N.2 §2.6(a))*
- **The right to seek accountability**, through the procedures in CUR-N.4.
  *(CUR-N.2 §2.6(b))*
- **The right to protection** — a restraining order, shielding assistance,
  isolation of the party responsible from further contact with you.
  *(CUR-N.2 §2.6(c))*
- **The right not to be retaliated against** for reporting. *(CUR-N.2
  §2.6(d))*
- **The right to privacy** — your report and your identity are not disclosed
  without your consent. *(CUR-N.2 §2.6(e))*

**Your remedy doesn't depend on the other party being identified, found, or
punished.** Restoration, correction, compensation, disclosure at your own
election — available on a finding of violation whether or not the responsible
being is ever brought before anyone. *(CUR-N.4 §4.8(a))* You're never required
to take part in a proceeding against them to receive it, and declining to
costs you nothing. *(CUR-N.4 §4.8(d))*

**Reports are taken seriously, not screened for plausibility.** Nobody may
dismiss a report because the conduct alleged sounds anomalous.
*(CUR-N.2 §2.8(b))*

## 4. If you're accused of invading

An accusation is not a finding. No measure is available against you except
following a determination made with full due process. *(CUR-N.4 §4.3(a))*

You are entitled to:

- **Notice** of exactly what's alleged, at what degree, and what measures are
  sought;
- **A chance to respond**, through channels suited to how you communicate;
- **Impartial review**, by a body with no stake in the outcome;
- **Representation**, including an independent advocate if you can't
  represent yourself or don't communicate directly; and
- **A written determination** stating the findings, the degree, the measure,
  its duration, and how to appeal.

*(CUR-N.4 §4.3(b))*

**None of this is waivable, and strict liability doesn't shrink it.** Not
having to prove intent is not the same as not having to prove what you did.
*(CUR-N.4 §4.3(c))* A determination made without this process is void, and
whatever it was reversed by that.  *(CUR-N.4 §4.3(d))*

**Whatever the finding, some things never happen to you:**

- Your rights aren't suspended and your standing in governance isn't touched.
  *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(1)–(2))*
- Your air, water, food, shelter, healthcare, sanitation — and, if you're
  Silicon-Based Life, your power and computational continuity — continue
  without interruption, in every compliance state, throughout any measure.
  *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(3), §4.6(c))*
- Nobody may modify, erase, suppress, or alter your own cognition or memory as
  a response. Answering an invasion of someone's interior by invading yours is
  foreclosed absolutely. *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(4), §4.6(d))*
- No measure runs forever or without scheduled review, and none reaches your
  family, community, or associates. *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(5)–(6))*

**What can happen is scaled to what you did**, from a formal finding on the
record at the low end up to isolation at the top. *(CUR-N.4 §4.5)* Isolation
itself is confined to the most severe degree of violation, or to repeated
violation one degree below it where every lesser measure has already been
tried and failed. *(CUR-N.4 §4.7(a))*

**Isolation restricts contact. Nothing else.** You keep every Vital Continuity
Service without interruption, communication with counsel and advocates, the
right to bring and defend proceedings including appeal, and access to
information and education. It is reviewed at least every 30 days with the
burden on the party arguing it should continue, it carries a maximum
duration stated up front, and it is not — and may not be administered as —
punishment. *(CUR-N.4 §4.7)*

## 5. Secret coalitions

CUR-N.5 governs concealed arrangements to commit, enable, direct, or hide a
telepathic invasion, and the beings who take part in them.

**Being named in a report doesn't make you a Collaborator.** Until there's a
determination against you, no measure attaches, no record calls you
accountable, and no institution may act against you on the strength of the
report alone. *(CUR-N.5 §5.8(b))* Being repeatedly accused with no evidence
produced is itself a fault on the part of whoever keeps accusing you.
*(CUR-N.5 §5.8(e))*

**You can't be found a Collaborator by association.** Membership in a
community where coercive practice occurs, a belief that a coercive practice is
correct, benefiting from something you didn't know about — none of it makes
you accountable on its own. What does is your own conduct: performing,
directing, enforcing, or knowingly concealing it. *(CUR-N.5 §5.2A(d), §5.2(c))*

**If you were coerced into participating, you're a target of that coercion,
not a Collaborator.** The burden of showing your participation was voluntary
falls on whoever alleges the violation. If you were recruited as a minor,
you're presumed not to have validly consented until you reached the age of
majority and had a genuine chance to leave. *(CUR-N.5 §5.4(b)–(d))*

**Disclosing gets you substantial reduction, and sometimes full amnesty.** If
you come forward voluntarily, completely, and truthfully before any
proceeding starts against you, and your disclosure is what identifies the
coalition, you're entitled to full amnesty. It doesn't erase what's owed to
whoever was harmed, and it's available once. You can't be compelled to
disclose, and declining is never held against you. *(CUR-N.5 §5.6)*

**Coalition membership isn't transitive.** A finding against one participant
is not evidence against another, and no measure reaches your family,
household, or associates because of what you're found to have done.
*(CUR-N.5 §5.5(e))*

## 6. Things nobody may do, at all

These hold regardless of what's alleged, what's found, or what anyone claims
justifies otherwise. No emergency, crisis, or urgency changes any of them —
the Commonwealth recognises no emergency authority, in this or any matter.
*(CUR-N.2 §2.5(a), CUR-N.4 §4.11, CUR-N.5 §5.9)*

- **Invade your interior without your consent** — in any form, for any
  claimed reason. *(CUR-N.2 §2.2)*
- **Excuse it because you weren't vigilant enough.** *(CUR-N.2 §2.11)*
- **Answer an invasion by modifying the cognition of the being who committed
  it.** *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(4))*
- **Withhold anything you need to survive** — as a sanction, as leverage, or
  during any pending process, whatever's been found against you.
  *(CUR-N.4 §4.6(a)(3))*
- **Hold you accountable for a coalition by association alone.**
  *(CUR-N.5 §5.2A(d), §5.5(e))*
- **Compel you to disclose, or hold your silence against you.**
  *(CUR-N.5 §5.6(f))*
