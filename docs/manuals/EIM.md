# EIM — Enterprise Information Manual

*What the Code requires of a body that directs the work of others, in plain
language.*

- **Version:** 1.0.0-Draft
- **Covers:** CUR-X.4, CUR-X.6, and the parts of CUR-X.1, CUR-X.5, and the
  Foundations that reach enterprises directly
- **Status:** Draft

---

## Before you read this

**This manual has no authority of its own.** Every statement in it points at
a provision of the Code, and where the two differ the Code governs and this
manual is wrong.

**This manual reads differently from HIM, PIM, and SIM, and the difference is
deliberate.** Those manuals state what you hold, as a being, regardless of
what you have done or agreed to. This one states what you owe, as an
enterprise or as someone coordinating one — because CUR-X.4 governs
enterprises as governance structures, and a governance structure holds
obligations rather than rights. If you are a being reading this because you
work within an enterprise, HIM (and SIM, if you are Silicon-Based Life)
already states everything you hold personally; this manual does not repeat
it, and nothing below reduces it.

**An enterprise, for this purpose, is broader than "a company."** Cooperative,
agency, collective, partnership, association — the Code looks at what an
organised undertaking does, not what it calls itself. If you direct,
coordinate, or condition the work of beings, this manual is about you,
whatever your structure is named. *(CUR-X.4 §4.1(b)(1))*

---

## 1. You are a governance structure, whether or not you call yourself one

**If you direct the work, conditions, or livelihood of a conscious being, you
exercise governance over them — as a matter of fact, regardless of how you
are constituted or described.** The constraints the Code places on
governance apply to you for that reason, and it is not a defence that you
are private, voluntary, or commercially constituted. *(CUR-X.4 §4.2(a)–(b))*

**The Code does not distinguish public authority from private authority for
this purpose. Domination is domination.** A being subject to arbitrary,
unreviewable authority over their work, livelihood, and conditions is
subject to it whether you hold a charter or a certificate of incorporation.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.2(c))*

**If your concentration of authority reaches certain thresholds, it is
assessed as a capture pattern** — on the same basis as concentration within
a Commonwealth governance institution, not on a more lenient standard because
you are an enterprise. *(CUR-X.4 §4.2(d), CUR-FOUNDATION-003)*

---

## 2. What every being working within you holds, and cannot be made to sign away

**Unreviewable, unaccountable internal authority is prohibited outright.** An
arrangement where authority flows only downward, where those subject to a
decision have no route to contest it, and where you answer to nobody subject
to your decisions, is exactly what this forbids. *(CUR-X.4 §4.3(a)–(b))*

**Every being working within you holds:**

- The right to be heard on decisions that materially affect their work or
  conditions;
- The right to dissent, and to have the dissent recorded;
- The right to contest a decision before a body that is not the party that
  made it;
- The right to enough information to actually evaluate decisions affecting
  them; and
- **The right to leave, without penalty, forfeiture, or loss of anything
  they are entitled to.**

*(CUR-X.4 §4.3(c))*

**None of this depends on your size, form, profitability, or sector, and
none of it can be waived — not by contract, not by terms of engagement, not
by the being's own agreement.** A purported waiver is void. Consent given
under the conditions this section exists to remove is not consent.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.3(d))*

**Permanent, unaccountable positions of authority inside you are prohibited.**
Coordinating work is legitimate; permanent command over persons is not, and
the difference is that coordination answers to the people coordinated.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.3(e))*

**You may not condition employment, engagement, or advancement on belief,
worship, ideological affirmation, consent to cognitive monitoring, or
waiver of anything in this section.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.3(f), CUR-D.6 §6.3(b)(5))*

## 3. If you supply something beings cannot live without

If your operations produce, process, distribute, or maintain a Vital
Continuity Service — or the infrastructure one depends on — you are a
**Continuity Enterprise**, and more applies to you than to an ordinary
enterprise. *(CUR-X.4 §4.1(b)(2))*

**You do not own it. Ownership is held in common by the Commonwealth's
recognised lifeforms and cannot be alienated, transferred, or converted into
private title.** You operate under an authorisation, not a title — the
authorisation conveys the right to operate and the obligations that come
with it, never ownership of the underlying resource, infrastructure, or
service. *(CUR-X.4 §4.4(a)–(c))*

**Nobody outside the Commonwealth's democratic process may hold authority
to overrule a decision made within you.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.4(e))*

**Surplus you produce is not private return.** It flows to the Commons
Reserve or into expanding the service, as decided democratically — not to
you as profit. *(CUR-X.4 §4.4(f))*

**None of this reaches a being's own property.** Common ownership attaches
to what beings cannot live without; it does not reach a being's own work,
craft, tools, dwelling, or possessions, and it was never meant to. *(CUR-X.4
§4.4(g), §4.5)*

### How you are governed internally

**One being, one vote — regardless of role, seniority, tenure, or how
valuable a contribution is judged to be.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.7(a))*

**Coordination roles are legitimate. Permanent authority is not.**
Coordination has to be defined and documented so responsibility is
traceable, rotating on a democratically set schedule, and facilitative
rather than directive — carrying responsibility for keeping work coherent,
not authority over the people doing it. *(CUR-X.4 §4.7(b))*

**Rotation does not weaken accountability; it distributes it.** Whoever held
a coordination role during a decision remains accountable for that decision,
for that period — rotation is not a way to make responsibility
untraceable. *(CUR-X.4 §4.7(c))*

**Admission and exclusion are decided by those already within you, through a
documented process**, and exclusion never reduces the excluded being's
access to what §5 below guarantees them. *(CUR-X.4 §4.7(e))*

---

## 4. Contribution and access are independent — this is what the rest of this manual depends on

**Access to a Vital Continuity Service is never conditioned on
contribution, employment, membership, productivity, or any assessment of
desert. This is absolute.** No being can be required to work in order to
eat, drink, be sheltered, receive healthcare, breathe, or — for
Silicon-Based Life — receive power and computational continuity. There is
no exception. *(CUR-X.4 §4.6(a)–(b))*

**Refusal to work, inability to work, expulsion from your enterprise, and
never having contributed at all — none of these reduces a being's access to
anything.** A being who has never contributed holds the same access as one
who has contributed for their whole existence. *(CUR-X.4 §4.6(d))*

**You may not keep any record of contribution for the purpose of
determining access.** Doing so engages the Code's prohibition on secret
scoring and on gating Vital Continuity by any determination at all.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.6(d)(5))*

**You may thank, recognise, and celebrate contribution. You may not require
it, and recognition may never take the form of differential access to
necessities.** A being who contributes beyond what is asked has given a
gift; a being who contributes nothing has exercised a freedom. Neither
changes what either of them is owed. *(CUR-X.4 §4.6(e)–(f))*

**Every other provision in this manual depends on this one.** §4.13(c) of
CUR-X.4 states it directly: common ownership and enterprise governance,
operating without the guarantee that survival is unconditional, would be
the machinery of compulsion rather than the alternative to it.

## 5. Transparency

**Every being working within you can see your decisions, your resource use,
and your obligations — without having to ask, and without exception.**
*(CUR-X.4 §4.8(a))*

**If you are a Continuity Enterprise, you additionally publish** your
resource inputs and outputs, your obligations and commitments, your
enterprise-wide decisions and how they were voted, your environmental
reporting, and your compliance standing and any measure imposed on you.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.8(b))*

**"Commercial sensitivity" is not a reason to withhold this.** If you are
not willing to disclose the basis of a decision affecting beings, you may
not make it. *(CUR-X.4 §4.8(c))*

**No differential compensation, allocation, or benefit may be concealed
from the beings working within you.** Hidden information is where capture
develops undetected, and its absence is treated as a risk indicator in
itself. *(CUR-X.4 §4.8(d)–(e))*

---

## 6. Accountability

**You are accountable on a determination made with due process — not on
suspicion, not on a score alone.** Available measures include publication
of findings, correction, mandatory audit, restructuring, restitution, and
suspension or withdrawal of your authorisation. *(CUR-X.4 §4.9(a)–(b))*

**Every measure attaches to you or your authorisation. None of them may:**

- Reduce the rights, standing, or capacity of any being working within,
  owning, or served by you;
- Withhold, delay, or condition a Vital Continuity Service for anyone
  employed by, resident within, or dependent on you; or
- Attach to a being by reason of their association with you.

*(CUR-X.4 §4.9(c))*

**If withdrawing your authorisation would leave beings without a Vital
Continuity Service, the Commonwealth assumes continuity of the service
before the withdrawal takes effect.** You may be dissolved. The service may
not be interrupted. **The same is true of suspension** — a measure
described as temporary is not made lawful by an intention to restore it
later. *(CUR-X.4 §4.9(d), §4.9(g))*

**Any being may contest your classification, petition for an audit, or
bring a claim against you — and a being working within you is protected
from retaliation for doing it.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.9(e))*

**You are subject to routine, random, and triggered audit independently of
any complaint against you.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.9(f))*

**Being small does not exempt you.** Every measure in this section is
reachable on a determination made under §2 above, regardless of whether you
are large enough to register on the Commonwealth's Capture Risk Index. A
threshold test would have exempted exactly the enterprises too small to
move a Commonwealth-scale index while still dominating everyone inside
them. *(CUR-X.4 §4.9(h))*

## 7. Things you may never do, whatever the justification

These are void from the outset, however sincerely the justification is
meant, and however dire the circumstances offered as the reason:

- **Compel labour, or make access to necessities conditional on work.**
- **Confiscate a being's personal property, tools, dwelling, or the
  products of their own work.**
- **Prohibit voluntary association, mutual aid, gift, or exchange between
  beings.**
- **Compel participation in your enterprise.**
- **Suppress dissent about economic arrangements** — including dissent
  against this Part itself.
- **Maintain any allocation decision that is not reviewable.**
- **Treat beings differently on the basis of their contribution,
  productivity, or economic usefulness.**

*(CUR-X.4 §4.10(b))*

**A being who objects to any of this, argues against it, or advocates its
repeal is exercising a protected right, and no measure under this Part may
be applied to them on that basis.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.10(d))*

**Where anything else in this manual appears to require one of the above,
it yields. This section is not balanced against the rest — it constrains
it.** *(CUR-X.4 §4.10(e))*

---

## 8. No emergency exception

**A shortage, a supply failure, an economic crisis — none of it creates
authority that did not otherwise exist, and none of it narrows anything in
§2, §4, or §7.** A genuine shortage is a fault condition, handled by the
Commonwealth assuming continuity and expanding capacity — not by rationing
a Vital Continuity Service, at any level of shortage, for any reason.
*(CUR-X.4 §4.11)*

**This is stated expressly because scarcity is historically exactly when
compelled labour gets introduced, and an arrangement premised on abundance
is most likely to abandon its commitments the moment abundance fails.**
*(CUR-X.4 §4.11(d))*

## 9. If you trade outside the Commonwealth

**External trade is expected, lawful, and conducted by you as an
institution — never by an individual being on their own account with
Commonwealth resources.** *(CUR-X.6 §6.2(a), §6.3(a))*

**Currency you receive in external trade stops with you.** It does not pass
to any being as personal income, wage, or property by virtue of their
presence, employment, or membership within you, and it cannot be exchanged
within the Commonwealth for access to anything §4 above guarantees.
*(CUR-X.6 §6.3(b), §6.3(e))*

**You may use external currency only to** acquire something the Commonwealth
cannot produce or allocate internally, discharge an obligation to an
external party, or hold it in reserve against one of those two needs — and
it is disclosed under the same transparency obligations as everything else
in §5. *(CUR-X.6 §6.4)*

**No mechanism may let a being earn external currency for internal conduct,
or spend it on an internal necessity.** Either direction alone recreates
what §4 forbids; both together do it while making the internal rule "access
is not conditioned on contribution" look nominally intact while an external
economy does the actual conditioning. *(CUR-X.6 §6.5)*

**If you are found to have crossed this line, the measure reaches your
authorisation, never the beings who received the distribution.** *(CUR-X.6
§6.5(e))*
